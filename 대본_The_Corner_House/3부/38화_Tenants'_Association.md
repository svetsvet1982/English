# THE CORNER HOUSE — 3부 38화

## Tenants' Association (세입자 협회)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 3부 2화(통산 38화) · 시기: 2026년 9월 중순 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 4B 프리야와 샘의 집 → 2B 에스포지토 부인의 집(협회 세 번째 회의)
> - 등장인물: SAM, PRIYA, MRS. ESPOSITO, EDDIE, MARISOL
> - 사건: 관리 회사가 같은 날 모든 세대에 '자발적 이주 패키지'를 보낸다. 에디 4만, 부인 3만 5천, 마리솔 1만 2천, 결혼을 앞둔 프리야·샘 1만 달러. 결혼 비용이 걱정인 샘과 "이웃을 팔지 말라"는 부인이 부딪치고, 마리솔이 '네 가지 규칙'으로 합의를 이끈다.
> - 학습 포인트: 토론 표현(I hear you, but… / That's not fair. / Let me propose something.), 합의 화법, 표결, 말 끊기와 사과
> - 미국 현안: 바이아웃의 계산법(일시금 vs 평생 오를 월세), 정당 사유 퇴거 보호법(10세대 넘게 가진 집주인에게 적용, 인상률 상한), 무료 세입자 법률 지원
> - 연결: 세입자 변호사 브룩스와 10월 면담 → 45화. 협회 공동 답신. 샘의 식기세척기 농담.

## 대본

[Scene: Tuesday evening. Apartment 4B. Sam holds a letter.]

SAM: Priya. Come look at this. From Bridgewater.
PRIYA: Another letter? What now? The inspection schedule?
SAM: No. It's... an offer. A "voluntary relocation package."
PRIYA: For us? We're market rate. Our lease ends in December anyway.
SAM: Read it. "We are pleased to offer you ten thousand dollars. If you move out by October thirty-first."
PRIYA: Ten thousand dollars? To leave two months early?
SAM: Ten thousand. That's half of the wedding. More than half.
PRIYA: Sam. We said we wanted to stay. We told everyone on the roof.
SAM: I know. I know. But think about it. In December, they can raise our rent. A lot. Maybe they won't even renew.
PRIYA: Marisol said Good Cause Eviction might protect us.
SAM: Might. "Might" doesn't pay for a wedding.
PRIYA: And where would we go? In October? With a wedding to plan?
SAM: My cousin says Jersey City has apartments. Bigger. With a dishwasher.
PRIYA: A dishwasher. You want to leave our friends for a dishwasher.
SAM: That's not fair. I want to leave a fight we might lose. For a sure thing.
PRIYA: There's a meeting tonight. At Dolores's. Let's go. Let's listen first.
SAM: And then?
PRIYA: And then we decide. Together. Not tonight. Not alone.
SAM: Okay. Together. But I'm bringing the letter.
PRIYA: Bring the letter. Everybody will have one.

MRS. ESPOSITO: Sam, if you leave, they renovate your apartment. They rent it for five thousand. Then the next one leaves. And the next.

MRS. ESPOSITO: It is if you live in a building!

MARISOL: Okay. Stop. Everyone, stop. We're not going to fight each other. That's exactly what they want.

MRS. ESPOSITO: All in favor?

PRIYA: In favor.
MARISOL: In favor. Sam?

MRS. ESPOSITO: Written. "With the association's help."
SAM: Okay. Then yes. Unanimous.
EDDIE: And Tommy? He's not here. The bar is busy.
MARISOL: Tommy texted me. "Count me in. Whatever you decide. And tell Eddie not to take the forty."
EDDIE: Ha! Tell Tommy I'm not taking the forty. I'm taking the stairs. Every day. Until I die.
PRIYA: Eddie!
EDDIE: What? It's true. Fifty-two years of stairs. That's my plan.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Okay. Next item. How do we answer the letters?
MARISOL: One letter. From the association. For everybody. "We received your offers. Our members are reviewing them with legal counsel. Please direct all communication to the association."
SAM: Legal counsel. That sounds serious.
MARISOL: That's the point. We want them to know we're serious.
EDDIE: Will they stop sending offers?
MARISOL: No. They'll send more. Maybe bigger. Maybe with pressure. Phone calls. Visits.
MRS. ESPOSITO: If they call me, I'll tell them to call the association.
PRIYA: And if they knock on our door?
MARISOL: Be polite. Take the paper. Say "I'll review it with my lawyer." And close the door.
SAM: "I'll review it with my lawyer." I like how that sounds.
EDDIE: I'm going to say it to everybody. To the mailman. To the cat.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The cat already has a lawyer. Me.
PRIYA: Sam, are you okay?
SAM: I'm okay. I think we're doing the right thing. Even if I'm still thinking about the dishwasher.
PRIYA: Our next apartment will have a dishwasher. This one. After we win.
SAM: After we win. Okay. I can live with that.
MARISOL: Meeting adjourned. Thank you, everyone. We did good tonight.
EDDIE: We did good. And nobody threw a plate. That's a win.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **토론 표현** — *I hear you, Sam. It is different for you. But not completely.*(상대 말을 인정하고 반론) / *That's not fair.* / *That's not my responsibility.* / *Let me propose something.* / *That's exactly what they want.*
- **말 끊기와 사과** — *Whoa. Selling my neighbors? That's not—* / *Okay. Stop. Everyone, stop.* / *I'm sorry, Sam. I'm scared. When I'm scared, I get loud.*
- **합의 화법** — *Not a decision for each person. A rule for all of us. / Whatever you decide, you don't decide alone. / Every member keeps the right to make their own choice.*
- **표결·회의 표현** — *Order, please. / Next item. / All in favor? / Unanimous. / Meeting adjourned.*(회의를 마칩니다)
- **a voluntary relocation package / a buyout / a deadline / six figures** — 자발적 이주 패키지 / 바이아웃 / 마감 / 여섯 자리 숫자(10만 달러 이상).
- **a sure thing** — 확실한 것.
- **It's a trap. With money inside.** — "덫이야. 안에 돈이 든."
- **legal counsel / housing court** — 법률 대리인 / 주택 법원.
- **Please direct all communication to…** — "모든 연락은 ~로 해 주십시오." 공문 표현.
- **I'll review it with my lawyer.** — "변호사와 검토하겠습니다." 압박을 받을 때 쓰는 안전한 대답.
- **Now I've heard everything.** — "별일 다 보겠네."
- **Count me in.** — "나도 끼워 줘, 찬성이야."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **I hear you, | but…** — hear you에서 고개를 끄덕이듯 내리고 쉰 다음 but을 분명히.
- 샘과 부인의 충돌은 점점 빨라지다가 마리솔의 *Okay. Stop.*에서 정지. 그 뒤로는 모두 목소리가 낮아진다.
- **voluntary** [**발**런테리], **relocation** [릴로우**케**이션], **exemption** [이그**젬**션], **adjourned** [어**저**r언드].
- 숫자: *forty thousand, thirty-five thousand, nine eighty-two, thirty-two hundred, twenty-two hundred*. 비교되는 숫자는 앞 숫자에 강세.
- 에디의 *I'm taking the stairs. Every day. Until I die.*는 웃으며 단호하게.
