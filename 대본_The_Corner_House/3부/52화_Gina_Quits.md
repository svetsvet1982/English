> - 등장인물: GINA, CRAIG, BRAD, LAUREN, OMAR, TOMMY, TASHA
> - 사건: 크레이그가 지나에게 '에이전트 우선 팀' 계획서를 맡긴다. 계획서대로라면 디자이너 로런의 자리가 사라진다. 지나는 쓰지 않겠다고 하고, 그 자리에서 2주 뒤 퇴사를 통보한다. 크레이그와 CEO 브래드가 연봉 인상과 직함으로 붙잡지만 지나는 거절한다. 다음 일은 다운타운 브루클린의 작은 비영리 단체 '오픈 블록'(도서관·주민 단체용 기술)이다. 주 4일 근무에 연봉은 20% 적다. 로비에서 로런이 고마워하고, 오마르가 전화로 응원한다. 밤에 바에서 토미에게 알린다.

[Scene: Thursday, 10:00 a.m. A small glass meeting room at Brightline, Midtown. Craig has a laptop open.]

GINA: Change into what, Craig?

GINA: So you want me to write the plan that removes Lauren.
CRAIG: I want you to write a plan for the future. Lauren is part of the present.
GINA: She has a one-hour commute, Craig. Each way. For five years. She's the best designer I've ever worked with.
CRAIG: I know. I like Lauren. But this isn't personal.
GINA: It is personal. For Lauren, it's very personal.
CRAIG: Look. If you don't write it, someone else will. At least you'll do it fairly.
GINA: That's what you said about Omar. "At least we'll do it fairly." Fifteen minutes with HR. Laptop locked.
CRAIG: That's not fair, Gina.
GINA: Maybe not. But it's true. I'm sorry. I won't write it.
CRAIG: You won't write it. Do you understand what that means? With Brad?

CRAIG: Okay. Go ahead.

CRAIG: Wait. What? You're quitting? Because of one plan?
GINA: Not because of one plan. Because of the last six months. The plan is just the last straw.
CRAIG: Gina, slow down. Let's talk about this. Is it money?
GINA: It's not money.
CRAIG: Is it the five days in the office? Because I can get you Fridays at home. I can ask.
GINA: It's not the Fridays. I should have said this in September. I should have said it when Omar left.
CRAIG: Do you have another job?
GINA: Yes. I accepted an offer yesterday. A small nonprofit. In Downtown Brooklyn. It's called Open Block.
CRAIG: Open Block? I've never heard of it.
GINA: Nobody has. They build simple tech for libraries. Tenant groups. Food pantries. Twelve people.
CRAIG: Twelve people. Gina, you lead product at a company with four hundred.
GINA: I lead product at a company that's trying to have forty.
CRAIG: That was honest. You were always honest. Too honest, Brad says.
BRAD: Knock knock. Craig, Gina. Is this a bad time? I saw your faces through the glass.
CRAIG: Brad. Gina just gave her notice.
BRAD: You're kidding. Gina, no. You're one of our best people.
GINA: Thank you, Brad. I appreciate that.
BRAD: Is there anything we can do to change your mind? Seriously. Name it.
GINA: I don't think so. I've made my decision.
BRAD: Fifteen percent. A raise. Starting January. And a new title. Senior Director of Product.
CRAIG: That's a really good offer, Gina.
GINA: It is a good offer. I appreciate it. But my answer is still no.
BRAD: Can I ask why? Honestly?
GINA: Honestly? You asked me to write a plan to replace a person I respect. With software. And I couldn't.
BRAD: That's business, Gina. Every company is doing it. If we don't, we lose.
GINA: Maybe you're right. Maybe that's business. But it's not my business. Not anymore.
BRAD: Okay. I respect that. I don't agree. But I respect it.
GINA: That's fair. Thank you.
BRAD: Will you finish the launch before you go? The December release?
GINA: I'll write a full handoff document. Every project. Every contact. You'll be in good hands.
BRAD: One page?
GINA: Four pages, Brad. Some things need four pages.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **사직 화법**
  - *I'm giving my two weeks' notice.* — "2주 뒤 퇴사를 통보합니다." 미국 직장의 관행.
  - *My last day will be November twenty-fifth.* — "마지막 근무일은 11월 25일입니다."
  - *I accepted an offer.* — 다른 회사 제안을 수락했다.
  - *I've made my decision.* — "결정했어요." 단호하지만 공손하다.
  - *I'll write a full handoff document.* — 인수인계 문서를 쓰겠다.
- **붙잡기와 거절**
  - *Is there anything we can do to change your mind? / Name it.* — "마음을 바꿀 방법이 있을까요? 말만 해요."
  - *I appreciate it. But my answer is still no.* — 감사를 먼저, 거절은 그다음에.
  - *I respect that. I don't agree. But I respect it.* — 동의하지 않아도 존중한다.
- **후회 표현**
  - *I should have said this in September.* — 그때 말했어야 했는데 (안 했다).
  - *I could have warned Omar.* — 경고할 수도 있었는데 (안 했다).
  - *I shouldn't have bought that jukebox.* — 사지 말았어야 했는데 (샀다).
  - *You couldn't have saved Omar.* — 네가 구할 수는 없었을 거야.
- **the last straw** — 결정타, 마지막 한 방울.
- **Quick-ish.** — "빠르게…쯤?" -ish는 '대략'.
- **Off the record?** — "비공식적으로 말해도 돼?"
- **put your name on the table** — 네 이름을 추천하겠다.
- **Money comes back. Years don't.** — "돈은 돌아와. 세월은 안 돌아와."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **should have → 'shoulda'** [슈더브/슈다]: *I should have said this* [아이 슈더 세드 디스]. *could have* → [쿠다], *shouldn't have* → [슈든어].
- 결심문은 하강 억양으로 끝을 단단히: *I've made my decision.↘ / My answer is still no.↘*
- 브래드의 제안은 빠르고 자신만만하게: *Fifteen percent. A raise. Starting January.*
- 지나가 로런에게 말할 때는 목소리를 낮춘다(*Off the record?*).
- 오마르의 *Money comes back. | Years don't.* — 짧은 두 문장. 사이에서 쉰다.
- 토미의 *Small regrets. | Big decisions.* — 대칭 구조, 강세는 *small*과 *big*.
