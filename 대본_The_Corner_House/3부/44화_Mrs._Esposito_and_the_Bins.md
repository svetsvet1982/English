# THE CORNER HOUSE — 3부 44화

## Mrs. Esposito and the Bins (에스포지토 부인과 쓰레기통)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 3부 8화(통산 44화) · 시기: 2026년 10월, 목요일 아침 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 건물 앞 인도 (아침 8시 → 오전 10시)
> - 등장인물: MRS. ESPOSITO, TOMMY, DIEGO, KOWALSKI, MARISOL, MARCUS
> - 사건: 현관에 위생국 벌금 딱지가 붙고, 쥐가 뜯어 놓은 쓰레기 봉투가 인도를 덮었다. 관리 회사는 세입자 탓을 하지만, 봉투 속에는 페인트 통과 석고 조각이 있다. 마커스 가게의 카메라가 새벽 2시에 봉투를 버리고 간 공사 업체 밴을 잡아낸다.
> - 학습 포인트: 규칙 설명, 비난과 반박(I didn't put it there / You can't be sure), 수동태(was left / were opened / was given / be fined)
> - 미국 현안: 뉴욕시 쓰레기통 의무화(9세대 이하 건물은 뚜껑 있는 통), 음식물 쓰레기 분리배출 의무화와 벌금, 시의 쥐 대책, 리모델링 공사 폐기물
> - 연결: 3B 리모델링 시작(공사 업체 Prime Build Contractors). 토미는 상가 쓰레기를 민간 수거 업체에 월 1,100달러 내고 처리. 2화의 마커스 카메라가 다시 활약. 코왈스키는 새 공식 쓰레기통·음식물 통·이번 주 방역을 서면으로 약속.

[Scene: Thursday, 8:00 a.m. The sidewalk in front of the building. Torn black bags. Trash everywhere.]

MRS. ESPOSITO: "Improper disposal. Trash bags placed at the curb without a lidded bin." It's a fifty-dollar fine.
TOMMY: Fifty dollars. So who has to pay it?
MRS. ESPOSITO: The owner, because it's addressed to the owner. But that's not the point. Look at the sidewalk!
DIEGO: Ew! The bags are all ripped open, and there's garbage everywhere!
MRS. ESPOSITO: The rats did that last night. I heard them under my window, scratching and fighting until three.
TOMMY: Rats. Great. Just what we need.
DIEGO: Wait, is that a rat? There, it's running under the car!
MRS. ESPOSITO: Diego, don't go near it, because they bite. And then you need shots.
DIEGO: It was huge! It was like a cat, but a cat with a long tail.
TOMMY: Okay, okay, everybody calm down. Let me go get a broom and some gloves.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Tommy, wait a minute. Before you clean anything, I want to know whose bags these are.
TOMMY: How should I know? They're black bags, Dolores, and all black bags look the same.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Well, I know my bags. I use the clear ones for recycling, and I put my food scraps in the brown bin, like the law says.
DIEGO: What law? There's a law about garbage?
MRS. ESPOSITO: It's the composting law. Food scraps go in a separate bin, so that means coffee grounds, banana peels, and eggshells. If you don't separate them, you get fined.
DIEGO: You can get fined for banana peels?
MRS. ESPOSITO: For banana peels in the wrong bag, yes. The city is very serious about it now.
TOMMY: Dolores, I know the rules. You've told me many times, usually while I'm trying to open the bar.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Then why is there a pizza box in this bag, with cheese stuck on it? And coffee grounds all over it?
TOMMY: A pizza box? We don't even sell pizza.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The bar has food, and the bar has coffee. Very bad coffee.
TOMMY: My bar trash never goes out here. I pay a private company, and they pick up the commercial trash from the back every night.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Every single night? Are you sure about that?
TOMMY: I pay eleven hundred dollars a month for it, so yes, I'm very sure.
DIEGO: Then whose trash is it?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Look inside. There are paint cans, pieces of wood, and chunks of plaster. That's not a family's trash.
TOMMY: You're right. That's construction trash.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Construction trash, in our building. So who's doing construction here?
TOMMY: Harborline is, in Three B. The workers started on Monday, and they've been banging all week.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The workers. Of course it's the workers.
DIEGO: Is that even allowed? Can you just put construction trash on the sidewalk?
TOMMY: No, it isn't. Construction debris has to go in a dumpster or a container, not in bags on the curb.
MRS. ESPOSITO: So they broke two rules. There was no bin, and they threw out construction debris.
TOMMY: And then the rats broke the bags.
MRS. ESPOSITO: That's it. I'm calling Bridgewater right now.
TOMMY: Call Kowalski. He fixes bikes, so maybe he can fix trash too.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **수동태로 책임 따지기** — 누가 했는지 모를 때나 행위 자체를 강조할 때. *The trash was left at the curb by someone in the building / The building was left without bins by the owner's workers. / The bags were opened by rats. / The ticket was given to the owner / You get fined.* by + 행위자로 책임을 드러내기도 한다(by rats, by the owner's workers).
- **비난과 반박** — *The owner thinks the tenants are responsible. / The tenants? Excuse me? / I didn't put it there. Do I look like I'm building a kitchen? / You can't be sure it was the contractor. / Then we don't know who did it. / We'll know soon enough.*
- **위생 규칙 어휘** — *a ticket*(벌금 딱지), *improper disposal*(부적절한 처리), *the curb*(도로 경계석, 쓰레기 내놓는 곳), *a lidded bin*(뚜껑 있는 통), *recycling*(재활용), *clear bags*, *food scraps / compost*(음식물 쓰레기 / 퇴비), *construction debris*(공사 폐기물), *a dumpster / a container*(대형 쓰레기 컨테이너), *an exterminator*(해충 방제업자), *pickup*(수거).
- **Just what we need.** — (반어) "딱 필요했던 거네(최악이네)."
- **Can I jump in?** — "제가 끼어들어도 될까요?"
- **I had a feeling you'd come.** — "오실 것 같더라고요."
- **I owe you an apology.** — "사과드려야겠네요."
- **move it up** — (일정을) 앞당기다.
- **They're going to have a bad day.** — "그쪽은 오늘 힘든 하루가 되겠네요(혼날 거예요)."
- **Done right.** — "제대로."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **수동태 문장의 강세**: *The trash was LEFT at the curb by someone in the building / The bags were OPENED by RATS.* — be동사는 약하게, 과거분사와 행위자에 강세.
- **I didn't put it THERE**처럼 대조 강세가 이 화의 리듬이다. *That's not a FAMILY'S trash. / It's the OWNER'S ticket, not yours.*
- **disposal** [디스**포**우절], **debris** [더**브리**], **exterminator** [익스**터**r미네이러r], **compost** [**캄**포우스트].
- 부인의 규칙 설명(*Trash goes in a bin with a lid…*)은 손가락을 꼽듯 하나씩 끊어서.
- 영상 장면은 실황처럼: *A white van. / Two guys get out. / Then they drive away.* 그리고 *...That's our contractor.*에서 코왈스키의 목소리가 작아진다.
