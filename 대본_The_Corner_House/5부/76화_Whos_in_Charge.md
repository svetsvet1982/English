# THE CORNER HOUSE — 5부 76화

## Who's in Charge? (누가 정하나?)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 5부 4화(통산 76화) · 시기: 2027년 4월 29일(목) 오후 → 5월 10일(월) 밤 · 난이도: B2~C1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 지하실(상자를 연다) → 소유주가 된 뒤 첫 이사회(문 닫은 더 훅) → 같은 자리, 관리인 코왈스키 합류
> - 등장인물: EDDIE, MRS. ESPOSITO, DIEGO, MARISOL, SAM, PRIYA, TOMMY, KOWALSKI
> - 사건: 디에고가 가져온 열쇠로 벽 속 상자가 열린다. 안에는 살의 1970~2019년 임대료 장부, 아내 루치아의 재봉 상자, 그리고 편지가 있다. "4C는 루치아의 재봉실이었다. 집이 없는 사람이 생기면 그 사람에게 줘라." 열하루 뒤 소유주로서 첫 이사회가 열리자 갈등이 터진다. 빈집 3B와 4C를 누구에게 줄지, 프리야의 강아지, 손님 숙박 규칙, 그리고 수리 순서다. 토미가 3B를 지나와 루카에게 주자고 하자 '가족 특혜' 논쟁이 붙는다. 관리인 코왈스키가 시 규제 약정을 설명한다. 빈집은 공개 추첨으로 배정해야 하고, 누구든 소득 기준 안에서 신청할 수 있다. 결국 규칙이 이긴다. 대신 4C의 우선 기준에 '집이 불안정한 사람'을 넣는다. 그날 밤, 반려동물 규칙은 '고양이 비스킷 조항'으로 정리된다.
> - 학습 포인트: 합의와 의견 대립(I hear what you're saying, but… / Let's agree on the process first. / That's not what I meant.), 정중하게 말 끊기(Sorry to interrupt, but… / Can I just jump in? / Hold that thought.), 양보(Fair enough. / I'll give you that. / Point taken.)
> - 미국 현안: 공동체 운영의 갈등, 적정 임대료 주택의 공개 추첨(공정 주택 원칙), 이해 충돌, 반려동물·손님 규정
> - 연결: 75화 상자·'4C — Sal's' 열쇠·5/3 새 보일러. 60화 토미가 지나 몫으로 준 2,500. 57화 정관(가구당 1표, 5천 초과 수리는 회원 표결). 19화 머피 신부가 집전한 살 부인의 장례. 59화 '2년 차 지붕' 빨간 칸. 72화 코왈스키가 커먼 그라운드로.

[Scene: Thursday, April 29, 4:05 p.m. The basement. Diego runs down the stairs with the old key. Marisol follows him. Eddie and Mrs. Esposito wait by the little metal door in the wall.]

DIEGO: I got it, I got it! The tag says "4C — Sal's," and I ran down four floors in forty seconds!
MARISOL: Eddie, what is all this? Diego said something about a secret box in the wall?
EDDIE: It's not secret, just hidden. It's been behind that pipe for maybe fifty years, and I never saw it once.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I told him we should wait for a lawyer before we opened anything. He refused, of course.
MARISOL: It's our building now, Dolores, so let's see what's inside. Diego, you found the key, so you get to open it.
DIEGO: Okay, the key goes in... and it turns! It's open! Whoa, it's heavy!
EDDIE: What's inside? Careful, Diego. Go slowly, because it's older than you are.
DIEGO: There's a big old book with a green cover, and a wooden box, and an envelope. It has writing on it.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Let me see the book first. It's a ledger, with the rent for every apartment, every month, since nineteen seventy, and it's all in pencil.
EDDIE: Look for 3A, will you? Try June nineteen seventy-four, because that's the month Peggy and I moved in.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Here it is, in Sal's handwriting: "3A. O'Neill, E. and P. June 1, 1974. Two hundred ten dollars. Paid."
EDDIE: Two hundred and ten dollars a month. Peggy wrote that check herself, with her good fountain pen.
DIEGO: Now look for Mom! Look for 4A, please!
MRS. ESPOSITO: 4A. "Reyes, M. September 2017. Fourteen hundred and five dollars. Paid." And next to it, in pencil, he wrote, "Good tenant. Baby boy. Loud."
MARISOL: He actually wrote that? About Diego, when he was a baby?
EDDIE: Sal wrote little notes about everybody in that book. I guess he thought nobody would ever read them.
DIEGO: "Loud"? That's not fair at all! I was a baby, and babies are supposed to be loud!
MRS. ESPOSITO: You're still loud, Diego, so Sal was right. He was always a very good judge of character.
DIEGO: And this box has needles, and thread, and tiny little scissors. It looks like a sewing kit.
MARISOL: There's a name carved into the lid. It says "Lucia."
EDDIE: Lucia was Sal's wife, and she sewed everything: dresses, curtains, and Halloween costumes for every kid on the block. She made one for Michael too, a pirate, back in nineteen seventy-nine.
MRS. ESPOSITO: She died in twenty twenty-one, and Father Murphy did the funeral. That's the same year 4C became empty.
MARISOL: And the envelope says, "To whoever owns this building after me."
EDDIE: Then you should read it, Marisol, because you're the owner now. Well, one of them.
MARISOL: "If you are reading this, you found Lucia's box. 4C was her sewing room, and after she died, I couldn't rent it. I couldn't let a stranger into her room."
MARISOL: "But a room should not stay empty when people have no home. When you are ready, give 4C to someone who needs it, someone with no place to go. That is what Lucia would want, because she always fed the kids who had no dinner. — Sal."
DIEGO: So Sal kept the apartment empty for her? For six whole years?
EDDIE: He did it for her, because he missed her so much. That stubborn old goat.
MRS. ESPOSITO: And then his memory started to go, so nobody knew why 4C was empty. Not even Richie knew.
MARISOL: Okay, this goes to the board, officially, as Sal's wish for 4C. Dolores, can you put it in the minutes?
MRS. ESPOSITO: It'll be item one on the new list, which is the owners' list. I'll call it "Lucia's room."

[Scene: Monday, May 10, 8:00 p.m. The Hook, closed. The first board meeting since the closing. Marisol, Sam, Priya, Mrs. Esposito, and Tommy sit at the long table. Agendas in front of everyone. Typed, of course.]

MARISOL: Okay, welcome to the first official meeting of the Corner House Co-op as owners. We have four items tonight: vacant units, pets, guests, and repairs. Let's try to be quick.
TOMMY: Quick-ish. Let's be realistic.
MARISOL: Fine, quick-ish. Item one is 3B and 4C, which are both empty. The renovations in 3B are almost finished, but 4C still needs a lot of work.
TOMMY: Can I start? I have an idea for 3B: Gina and Luca. Her lease in Carroll Gardens ends in August, so she could move right in. She's been with us since the very start.
PRIYA: Tommy, I love Gina, and everybody loves Gina. But...
TOMMY: But what?
PRIYA: But she's your daughter, and you're on the board. That's a conflict of interest, even if nobody means anything bad by it.
TOMMY: A conflict? She did the whole city application! She wrote forty-two pages, and she's the one who called the foundation!
SAM: Sorry to interrupt, but Priya's right. It's not about Gina; it's about the process. If the board gives an apartment to a board member's daughter, how does that look to everybody else?
TOMMY: It looks like we're thanking her, which we should.
MRS. ESPOSITO: It looks like what Richie did, Tommy. Family first, and rules later, if ever.
TOMMY: That's not fair, Dolores. That's not what I meant. You know that.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I know it's not what you meant. But it's what other people will see. And people remember.
TOMMY: Then what about Sal's letter, and 4C? He wrote, "Give it to someone who needs it." But who decides who needs it? Us?
MARISOL: Hold that thought, Tommy, because that's the real question tonight. Who decides?
PRIYA: Can I just jump in? Item two isn't any easier, because I want a dog. It would be a small one, and Sam said maybe.
SAM: I said "maybe after the wedding." That's very different.
MRS. ESPOSITO: A dog? In this building? Biscuit will have a heart attack.
PRIYA: Biscuit is a cat, Dolores, and a grown cat. She'll survive.
MRS. ESPOSITO: She's fourteen years old, and she has a weak heart. She also has very strong opinions.
TOMMY: And item three is guests. Jesse stays at Tess's place, and Tess stays at Jesse's place. And Sam's cousins stayed in 4B for nine days at Christmas.
SAM: It was eight days, not nine. And they brought you a cake, if you remember.
TOMMY: It was a good cake, I'll admit. But the bylaws say nothing about guests, so what's the limit? A week?
MARISOL: Okay, okay, everybody stop. We're arguing about four things at once, and we're getting personal. Let's agree on the process first.

PRIYA: Fair enough. I'll wait on the dog.

EDDIE: And what about Gina? Did you ask her what she wants before you fought for her?
TOMMY: ...No. I didn't ask her.
EDDIE: Then ask her. She might not even want 3B, because she might want her own life, ten minutes away.
TOMMY: You really think so?
SAM: She told Priya last week that she loves Carroll Gardens, and Luca's school is there. She was going to say no, Tommy.
TOMMY: She was going to say no? And I fought for an hour? Great. I'm a very good father, with very bad information.

MARISOL: Please do. Sam's been waiting all night.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **정중하게 말 끊기**
  - *Sorry to interrupt, but…* — "말씀 중에 죄송하지만…"
  - *Can I just jump in?* — "잠깐 끼어들어도 될까요?"
  - *Hold that thought.* — "그 생각 잠깐 붙잡아 두세요." 지금은 넘어가되 나중에 다루겠다는 뜻.
- **의견 대립과 합의**
  - *That's not what I meant.* — "그런 뜻이 아니었어."
  - *I know it's not what you meant. But it's what other people will see.* — 의도와 인상을 구분한다.
  - *Let's agree on the process first.* — "절차부터 합의하자." 내용보다 방법을 먼저.
  - *I hear what you're saying. I don't like it. But I hear it.* — 받아들이기 싫지만 이해는 한다.
- **양보**
  - *Point taken.* — "알겠어, 일리 있어."
  - *Fair enough.* — "그래, 그럴 만해."
  - *I'll give you that.* — "그건 인정할게."
- **운영 어휘**
  - *conflict of interest* 이해 충돌 / *open lottery* 공개 추첨 / *income limit* 소득 상한 / *preference* 우선 기준
  - *regulatory agreement* 규제 약정 / *right of way* 통행 우선권 / *neutral territory* 중립 지대 / *seniority* 연공서열
- **The stoop is Switzerland.** — "스툽은 스위스야(중립국)." 부인의 비유.
- **The rules are in charge. And the rules are us.** — 마리솔이 정리한 협동조합의 원칙.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- 첫 장면 상자를 여는 순간은 조용하고 느리게. 장부 기록은 또박또박: *"3A. O'Neill, E. and P. June 1, 1974. Two hundred ten dollars. Paid."*
- 살의 편지는 마리솔이 천천히 읽는다. 두 번째 단락에서 목소리를 조금 낮춘다.
- 회의 장면은 말이 겹칠 듯 빠르게. *Sorry to interrupt, but*는 *sorry*를 부드럽게, *but*에서 단단하게.
- 토미의 *That's not what I meant.*는 상처받은 목소리로. 부인의 대답은 따뜻하지만 물러서지 않게.
- 에디의 연설(*For fifty years, this building had one man in charge.*)은 한 문장씩 무게를 두고.
- 마지막 부인의 *One fifth. | Don't get excited.* — 건조한 유머.
