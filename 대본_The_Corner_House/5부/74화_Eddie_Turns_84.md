# THE CORNER HOUSE — 5부 74화

## Eddie Turns 84 (에디, 여든넷이 되다)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 5부 2화(통산 74화) · 시기: 2027년 3월 17일(수) 성 패트릭 데이 · 난이도: B2~C1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 오전의 더 훅 → 오후의 바(깜짝 방문) → 저녁 생일 파티 → 밤의 계단
> - 등장인물: EDDIE, TOMMY, TASHA, MRS. ESPOSITO, MICHAEL, KAYLA, DIEGO, MARISOL, GINA, JESSE
> - 사건: 1년 전 6화와 똑같이 에디가 초록 스웨터를 입고 바에 들어온다. "그냥 커피. 평일이잖아." 토미와 타샤는 "작년 이맘때"를 하나씩 꺼낸다. 피처폰, 파란 자전거, 하버라인, 보일러, 그리고 매매 계약. 오후에 플로리다에서 아들 마이클과 손녀 케일라가 몰래 찾아온다. 저녁 파티에서는 디에고가 그 그림의 액자를, 제시가 '200만 조회 수' 인증서를, 부인이 코팅한 '두 번 두드리기' 카드를 선물한다. 작년 소원 "이 건물에서 1년 더"는 이루어졌다. 밤에 계단에서 마이클은 처음으로 이 계단이 왜 아버지의 세상인지 이해한다.
> - 학습 포인트: 회고 표현(This time last year… / A year ago, I'd never… / Since then… / Looking back… / Who would have thought…?), 생일 표현 복습(6화 재사용: Many happy returns. / Make a wish. / Blow out the candles.), 노년의 어조
> - 미국 현안: 고령 부모와 멀리 사는 자녀, '에이징 인 플레이스'(살던 곳에서 늙어 가기), 세대 간 기억
> - 연결: 6화 83세 생일(초록 스웨터, 부인의 1974년 사진, 소원 '이 건물에서 1년 더'+토미 커피, 'Danny Boy', 마이클의 스마트폰). 11화 스마트폰·케일라 사칭 사기. 64화 스케이트. 68화 마이클 '7만 5천 받으세요'. 70화 마이클 2천 기부. 73화 매매 수락. 3월 15일 매매 계약 서명.

## 대본

[Scene: Wednesday, March 17, 11:00 a.m. Eddie comes into The Hook wearing a bright green sweater. The same one as last year. Tommy is behind the bar. Tasha is cutting lemons.]

EDDIE: Morning, Tommy. Just coffee. It's a weekday.
TOMMY: Just coffee? On your birthday? On Saint Patrick's Day? Again?
EDDIE: Again. Some things change. Some things don't. My coffee order doesn't.
TASHA: Happy birthday, Eddie! Eighty-four! You look exactly the same as last year!
EDDIE: Same sweater. Different man.
TOMMY: Different how?
EDDIE: Let me think. This time last year, I had a flip phone from two thousand nine. Now I have a phone that sends hearts to Dolores.
TASHA: And you're internet famous. Two million views.
EDDIE: A year ago, I'd never heard the word "viral." I thought it was a disease.
TOMMY: It kind of is. Okay. My turn. This time last year, I was worried about the price of eggs. Remember? Seven dollars a dozen?
TASHA: And I was worried about my tips. Now I'm worried about a chemistry exam. On Friday. Night classes are hard.
EDDIE: Looking back, what was the biggest thing? This whole year?
TOMMY: Easy. A note under my door. May thirty-first. "The building is being sold." I almost threw up.
EDDIE: And since then?
TOMMY: Since then... Harborline. Bridgewater. Buyout letters. A flood. A boiler. Brian Kowalski. A co-op. And on Monday, we signed the purchase agreement. Forty pages. I signed fourteen times.
TASHA: Fifteen. I counted. You signed one page twice by mistake.
TOMMY: Who would have thought? A year ago, I didn't know what a quorum was.
EDDIE: A year ago, nobody knew what a quorum was. Except Dolores.
TASHA: Eddie, do you remember your wish? Last year? When you blew out the candles?
EDDIE: Of course. "One more year in this building." And a better cup of coffee.
TOMMY: One out of two. The building, you got. The coffee, never.
EDDIE: One out of two at my age is a perfect score.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Happy birthday, Eddie! I brought you something. But you can't open it until tonight.
EDDIE: Dolores. Thank you. And the knock this morning was very loud. Louder than usual.
MRS. ESPOSITO: It was a birthday knock. Three knocks. One extra for the birthday.
EDDIE: I knocked back four. One extra for the building.
TASHA: You two have a secret language now. With knocks. Like whales.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Not secret, Tasha. Everybody in the building can hear it. That's the point.

[Scene: 2:30 p.m. The Hook. Eddie sits at his stool reading the newspaper. The door opens. A man in his fifties and a young woman with a backpack walk in.]

MICHAEL: Excuse me. Is this the last real bar in Brooklyn? I'm looking for a famous skater.
EDDIE: Michael? Michael! What... what are you doing here?
MICHAEL: Happy birthday, Dad. Surprise.
KAYLA: Happy birthday, Grandpa! We flew in this morning! Seven a.m. from Tampa!
EDDIE: Kayla! Kayla, look at you! Come here! Both of you! Come here!
TOMMY: Michael O'Neill. I haven't seen you in ten years. You got gray.
MICHAEL: Tommy Russo. You got loud. Louder.
TOMMY: I was always loud. Now I'm a homeowner. Homeowners are louder.
EDDIE: Why didn't you tell me? I would have cleaned the apartment. I would have bought cake.
MICHAEL: Tommy told us not to tell you. And Mrs. Esposito. She planned everything. With a clipboard.
EDDIE: Dolores planned this? That woman. That sneaky, wonderful woman.
KAYLA: She emailed me a schedule, Grandpa. With times. "Two thirty, arrive. Two thirty-five, Eddie cries."
EDDIE: I'm not crying.
TOMMY: Two thirty-four. He's ahead of schedule.
MICHAEL: Dad. I watched the video. The skating. Forty times. My boss watched it. My wife cried. Kayla's whole dorm watched it.
KAYLA: My roommate made it her phone background. You're her phone background, Grandpa.
EDDIE: Her phone background? What does that mean? Am I inside her phone?
KAYLA: Kind of. Yes.
MICHAEL: And then I read about the boiler. And the offers. And I called you and told you to take the money. And come to Florida.
EDDIE: I remember. You were angry.
MICHAEL: I was scared. That's different. I was scared you'd be alone. On the third floor. At fifty-one degrees. And I was a thousand miles away.
EDDIE: I wasn't alone, son. That's what I tried to tell you. Dolores came up with the key. Tess came with a thermometer. Tommy opened the bar.
MICHAEL: I know that now. I see it now. That's why I came. To see it. With my own eyes.
TOMMY: Then stay for the party. Six o'clock. The whole building. You'll see everything.
KAYLA: We're staying until Sunday. Mrs. Esposito already booked us a room. At a bed-and-breakfast in Carroll Gardens. Gina's friend runs it.
EDDIE: Of course she did. Michael, Kayla. Sit. Sit with me. Tommy, two more coffees. Not your coffee. Tasha's coffee.
TOMMY: Tasha's coffee? On your birthday? You've been hiding Tasha's coffee from me?
EDDIE: Everybody has, Tommy. For a year. Behind the cereal.

[Scene: 6:30 p.m. Green balloons over the bar. Neighbors crowd in. Diego holds a flat package. Jesse holds a rolled-up paper. Marisol and Gina stand near the cake.]

GINA: Okay, everybody! Gifts first! Then cake! Then the song!
DIEGO: Me first! Eddie, open it! I made it! Well, I drew it. Mom paid for the frame.
EDDIE: What is it? It's heavy. Oh. Oh, Diego.
DIEGO: It's the drawing. From the bid. The real one. Mr. Whitlock sent it back. With a note.
MARISOL: He mailed it on Monday. With the signed contract. His note says, "This belongs to the building, not to Harborline."
EDDIE: Look at that. Me in the window. With my radio. Young. Like nineteen seventy.
DIEGO: And Peggy is there too. Look. On the roof. Next to Nadia. In a red coat. Skating backward.
EDDIE: You... you put Peggy on the roof?
DIEGO: She needed to be there. She's part of the building. Like you said.
EDDIE: Many happy returns, Diego. That's the best gift anybody ever gave me. Since the lake.
JESSE: Okay. Hard act to follow. But here. I made a certificate. Official. "Edward O'Neill. Two million, three hundred forty-one thousand views. Most famous man in Red Hook."
EDDIE: Two million three hundred! It went up!
JESSE: It goes up every day. A school in Japan used it in an English class. As a reading exercise.
EDDIE: Japan! Children in Japan are listening to me say "Push and glide"?
JESSE: With subtitles.
GINA: And from the building. From the co-op. This is not official yet. The closing is April thirtieth. But we made a sample. Share certificate number one.
EDDIE: Number one? Why me?
MARISOL: Because you've lived here the longest. Fifty-two years. You get share number one. We voted. Five to zero.
EDDIE: Five to zero. Again. This building agrees too much lately.
GINA: And this one is from Dolores. She's in the kitchen. Guarding the cake. Open it.
EDDIE: A card. Laminated. "Eight a.m. Two knocks. Reply: two knocks. If no reply: come up. Key: Dolores. Backup key: Marisol. Backup backup: Tommy."
GINA: She said: for your fridge. Next to Peggy's picture. So everybody knows the system. Even when she's gone.
EDDIE: Dolores is not going anywhere.
GINA: She said you'd say that. Her answer: "Neither is he. That's why it's laminated. It's built to last."
GINA: Okay! Cake! Jesse, the lights! Everybody, sing!
JESSE: Lights off! Ready!
GINA: Happy birthday to you... happy birthday to you...
DIEGO: Happy birthday, dear Eddie...
GINA: Happy birthday to you! Make a wish, Eddie!
EDDIE: Okay. A wish. Let me think. Last year, I wished for one more year in this building.
DIEGO: And you got it!
EDDIE: I got it. So this year... I wish for the same thing. One more year. In this building. In my building. And then, next year, I'll wish again.
JESSE: That's a renewable wish. Like a lease.
EDDIE: Like a lease. Exactly. A good lease. One that never runs out. Okay. Blow.
DIEGO: Eighty-four candles! You did all of them! In one breath!
EDDIE: Eighty-four candles. I used to blow out a match and get dizzy. I'm getting stronger.
DIEGO: Eddie, the song! "Danny Boy"! Like last year!
EDDIE: My voice is worse than last year.
MARISOL: Your voice is the tradition, Eddie. Sing.
EDDIE: "Oh, Danny boy, the pipes, the pipes are calling..."

[Scene: 11:00 p.m. The staircase between the second and third floors. Eddie sits on his step. Michael sits one step below him with two cups of tea that Mrs. Esposito sent up.]

MICHAEL: So this is the famous step. The halfway house.
EDDIE: The halfway house. Dr. Levine's idea. I rest here every time. Forty-two steps total. This is number twenty-one.
MICHAEL: I grew up on these stairs, Dad. I forgot. I used to jump down the last five. Mom yelled every time.
EDDIE: Every time. "Michael Patrick O'Neill, you'll break your neck!" Then you'd land like a cat. And she'd laugh.
MICHAEL: I'd forgotten that too. Her laugh. On the stairs.
EDDIE: The stairs remember. That's the thing about old buildings. They remember everything. Every footstep. Every fight. Every Christmas.
MICHAEL: Dad. I owe you an apology. For the phone call. In February. "Take the seventy-five thousand."
EDDIE: You don't owe me anything. You were scared. You're my son. You're allowed to be scared for me.
MICHAEL: But I didn't understand. I thought you were staying because you're stubborn. Like Grandpa. I thought it was pride.
EDDIE: It's a little pride. I won't lie.
MICHAEL: But it's not only pride. I saw it tonight. Dolores with her laminated card. Diego with the drawing. Tommy crying into the cake. Sam fixing the candle holder. Nobody in Florida knows my name like that. Not after twenty years.
EDDIE: It took fifty-two years. It doesn't happen fast. It happens one knock at a time.
MICHAEL: Can I say something? And you won't get mad?
EDDIE: People always say that. Then I always get mad. Go ahead.
MICHAEL: When you can't do the stairs anymore. And that day will come, Dad. Will you think about Florida? Just think?
EDDIE: ...I'll think about it. When that day comes. I promise to think. But I also want you to know something.
MICHAEL: What?
EDDIE: The co-op has a plan. For that day. There's a ground-floor apartment. Behind the bar. It's storage now. Sam put it in the five-year budget. "Accessible unit." No stairs.
MICHAEL: Sam planned for that? For you?
EDDIE: Sam plans for everything. It's his curse.
MICHAEL: Then I'm not worried anymore. Okay. A little. But less.
EDDIE: Less is good. Less is enough. Now help me up. Twenty-one more steps. Then sleep.
MICHAEL: Twenty-one more. Lean on me, Dad.
EDDIE: Happy birthday to me. This time last year, I climbed these stairs alone. This year, I have my son.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **회고 표현**
  - *This time last year, I had a flip phone.* — "작년 이맘때는 폴더폰을 썼지."
  - *A year ago, I'd never heard the word "viral."* — 과거완료로 '그때까지 한 번도'.
  - *Since then, …* — "그 뒤로…"
  - *Looking back, what was the biggest thing?* — "돌아보면 가장 큰 일은 뭐였어?"
  - *Who would have thought?* — "누가 생각이나 했겠어?"
- **생일 표현 복습(6화)**
  - *Many happy returns.* — "생일 축하해(이런 날이 여러 번 돌아오길)." 격식 있고 오래된 표현.
  - *Make a wish. / Blow out the candles.* — "소원 빌어. / 촛불 꺼."
- **Same sweater. Different man.** — "스웨터는 같고, 사람은 달라졌지."
- **One out of two at my age is a perfect score.** — "내 나이에 둘 중 하나면 만점이야."
- **a renewable wish. Like a lease.** — 갱신 가능한 소원. 임대 계약처럼.
- **halfway house** — 원래는 '사회 복귀 시설'. 에디는 계단 중간 쉼터를 농담으로 이렇게 부른다.
- **accessible unit** — 장애인·노인이 쓰기 쉬운(계단 없는) 집.
- **It happens one knock at a time.** — "하나의 노크씩 쌓여 이루어지는 거야."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- 6화와 같은 첫 대사 *Just coffee. It's a weekday.*를 똑같은 리듬으로. 듣는 사람이 1년 전을 떠올리게 한다.
- *This time last year*를 말할 때마다 약간 멈추고 그때를 떠올리듯.
- 마이클의 첫 등장은 장난스럽게, 에디의 *Michael? Michael!*은 두 번째에 크게 터진다.
- 생일 노래는 다 같이 부르듯, 'Danny Boy'는 에디 혼자 느리게.
- 마지막 계단 장면은 아버지와 아들의 낮은 대화. *Forty-two steps total.* 숫자는 담담하게.
- 마지막 문장 *This year, I have my son.* — 3월의 새벽처럼 조용하게.

타샤: 그리고 인터넷 유명인이시죠. 조회 수 200만.

토미: 쉽죠. 문 밑의 쪽지요. 5월 31일. "건물이 팔리고 있음." 토할 뻔했어요.
에디: 그 뒤로는?
토미: 그 뒤로는… 하버라인. 브리지워터. 바이아웃 편지. 홍수. 보일러. 브라이언 코왈스키. 협동조합. 그리고 월요일에 매매 계약에 서명했죠. 40쪽. 열네 번 서명했어요.

토미: 둘 중 하나. 건물은 얻으셨고. 커피는 절대 안 되죠.

에스포지토 부인: 생일 노크였어요. 세 번. 생일이라 한 번 더.
에디: 난 네 번으로 답했지. 건물 몫으로 한 번 더.
타샤: 두 분 이제 비밀 언어가 있네요. 노크로요. 고래처럼요.
