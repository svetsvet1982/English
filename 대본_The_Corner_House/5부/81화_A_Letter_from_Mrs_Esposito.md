# THE CORNER HOUSE — 5부 81화

## A Letter from Mrs. Esposito (에스포지토 부인의 편지)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 5부 9화(통산 81화) · 시기: 2027년 5월 20일(목) 밤 · 난이도: B2~C1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 루이스 가족이 떠난 뒤의 더 훅 → 바의 끝자리 → 2B 문 앞
> - 등장인물: MRS. ESPOSITO, GINA, TOMMY, EDDIE, TASHA
> - 사건: 열흘 전 첫 이사회에서 부인은 토미에게 "리치가 한 짓처럼 보인다"고 말했다. 그 뒤로 둘은 어색하다. 부인은 타자기로 편지를 쳤지만 직접 읽을 용기가 없다. 그래서 지나에게 봉투를 건넨다. "네 아버지에게 읽어 줘." 지나가 바에서 소리 내어 읽는다. 1994년에 세상을 떠난 남편 카르미네, 혼자 이 건물에 들어온 1995년, 클립보드와 규칙이 그녀에게 무엇이었는지, 그리고 2020년 봄 토미가 매일 문 앞에 두고 간 장바구니 이야기가 나온다. 편지 끝에는 부탁이 하나 있다. 내일 스툽 세일의 사회를 맡아 달라는 것이다. 이번이 그녀가 주최하는 마지막 스툽 세일이다.
> - 학습 포인트: 편지체(I am writing to… / I owe you an apology. / I was wrong to say… / I will never forget… / Please accept… / With gratitude, / Yours truly,), 사과·감사 표현, 편지 낭독 톤
> - 미국 현안: 혼자 사는 노년, 팬데믹의 기억(2020년 봉쇄와 이웃의 돌봄), 이웃 간의 화해, 지역 공동체 리더십의 세대교체
> - 연결: 76화 '리치가 한 짓처럼 보일 거예요'. 12·13화 첫 스툽 세일(디에고 자전거 저금). 35화 부인의 옥상 만찬. 58화 부인 MTA 급여과 1978~2008. 53화 '두 번 두드리기'. 80화 루이스 가족 방문.

## 대본

[Scene: Thursday, May 20, 9:30 p.m. The Hook. Luis's family has just gone home. Tamale wrappers on the tables. Tasha stacks glasses. Tommy wipes the bar. Eddie sits on his stool. Mrs. Esposito comes in holding a white envelope and goes straight to Gina at the end of the bar.]

MRS. ESPOSITO: Gina. Can I ask you a favor? A strange one?
GINA: Of course, Dolores. Anything.
MRS. ESPOSITO: This is a letter. For your father. I typed it. On my typewriter. Three drafts.
GINA: For Dad? Why don't you give it to him? He's right there.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Because I can't read it out loud. My voice will shake. And I'll stop. And then I'll say something sharp to cover it. That's what I do.
GINA: You want me to read it? To him?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Yes. Please. Out loud. Here. I'll sit next to Eddie. I'll listen. And I won't interrupt. For once.
EDDIE: That I'd like to see. Dolores, not interrupting.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Quiet, Eddie. I'm practicing.
EDDIE: What's in it? Can you tell me? A preview?
MRS. ESPOSITO: No previews. You'll hear it with everybody.
EDDIE: Is it about me?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Not everything is about you, Eddie. Only some things.
GINA: Dad. Can you come here? Dolores wrote you a letter. She wants me to read it.
TOMMY: A letter? To me? Am I in trouble? Is it about the shed?
MRS. ESPOSITO: It's not about the shed.
TASHA: Should I leave? It sounds private.
MRS. ESPOSITO: No, Tasha. Stay. Everybody heard what I said at the meeting. Everybody should hear this too.
TOMMY: Okay. Okay. I'm sitting. Go ahead, Gina.

[Scene: 9:40 p.m. The same place. Gina unfolds two typed pages. Tommy sits on a stool facing her. Mrs. Esposito sits beside Eddie with her hands folded. Tasha leans on the bar.]

GINA: "Dear Tommy. I am writing to say something I could not say at the board meeting. I owe you an apology."
GINA: "Ten days ago, you asked the board to give 3B to Gina and Luca. I said it would look like what Richie did. Family first. Rules later."
GINA: "I was wrong to say that. Not wrong about the rules. The rules were right. But I was wrong to compare you to Richie. You are nothing like Richie."
TOMMY: ...
GINA: "Richie sold this building to strangers to pay his debts. You fought for this building with everything you had. Even when you had nothing. You wanted to give your daughter a home. That is not corruption. That is love. I should have said that first."
TOMMY: Dolores...
MRS. ESPOSITO: Shh. Let her finish. There's more. Two pages.
GINA: "You may wonder why I care so much about rules. Why I have a clipboard. Why I count everything. I have never told anyone in this building. Not even Eddie."
EDDIE: She hasn't. I asked her once. In two thousand five. She changed the subject to the trash schedule.
GINA: "In nineteen ninety-four, my husband, Carmine, died. Very suddenly. A heart attack. On the subway. On his way home from work. He was fifty-one."
TASHA: Oh, Dolores. I didn't know.
GINA: "We had an apartment in Bensonhurst. In his name. Only his name. After he died, the landlord gave me thirty days. He said, 'The lease is not yours.' I had a job at the MTA. But no home. I was forty-four."
GINA: "A woman from church told me about a building in Red Hook. A landlord named Sal. I came here in the rain. In March nineteen ninety-five. I had one suitcase and Carmine's watch."
GINA: "Sal looked at me for a long time. Then he said, 'You need a place. I have a place. Two B. Pay me when you can.' I paid him on the first of every month for thirty-one years. Never late. Not once."
EDDIE: Never late. Sal told me. He said, "The widow in 2B pays before the bank opens."
GINA: "After that, I made a promise. Nobody would ever tell me again that something was not mine. So I wrote everything down. Every rule. Every date. Every receipt. The clipboard was my armor. It still is."
GINA: "Then, in March twenty twenty, the city closed. Everyone stayed inside. I was seventy. Alone. With a cat. I was afraid to go to the store. The news said people my age were dying."
GINA: "Every morning for three months, there was a bag at my door. Eggs, milk, bread, cat food, and one lemon. Always one lemon. No note. I never saw who left it."
TOMMY: ...You knew?
GINA: "I found out in June. I opened the door early. And I saw you. Running down the stairs. You had a mask on. And you were carrying four more bags. For Eddie. For Sal. For the family in 4A. And one for yourself."
EDDIE: Tommy. That was you? The lemons? I thought it was Michael. Ordering online from Florida.
TOMMY: Michael couldn't order a pizza online. It was me. Angela used to put a lemon in every bag. For luck. I just kept doing it.
GINA: "Tommy, I never thanked you. I was too proud. I'm thanking you now. I will never forget those bags. I will never forget the lemon."
GINA: "Please accept my apology. And please accept a favor. Tomorrow evening, we set up the stoop sale. Saturday is the sale. It will be my last one as president of the block association. Twenty years is enough."
TASHA: Your last one? Dolores!
GINA: "I would like you to be the master of ceremonies. You have a loud voice. And a big heart. Those are the only two things the job needs."
GINA: "With gratitude, and with respect. Yours truly, Dolores Esposito. 2B. P.S. Bring a lemon."

[Scene: 9:55 p.m. The same place. Gina folds the letter. Nobody speaks for a moment. Then Tommy stands up and walks to the end of the bar.]

TOMMY: Dolores.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Don't say anything sharp. I'll cry.
TOMMY: I'm not going to say anything sharp. I'm going to say thank you. And I'm sorry too.
MRS. ESPOSITO: You? Sorry for what?
TOMMY: For getting angry at the meeting. You were right. About the rules. About how it looked. I was being a father. Not a board member.
MRS. ESPOSITO: You were being both. That's allowed. It's just hard.
TOMMY: And I'm sorry about Carmine. Thirty-three years. And I never knew. You never said.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I never said because nobody asked. And I never asked anyone to ask. That's my fault.
EDDIE: It's nobody's fault, Dolores. You were busy. Running the block. Writing the minutes. Saving all of us.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I wasn't saving anybody, Eddie. I was saving myself. With paper.
EDDIE: Same thing. In the end. You saved yourself, and the rest of us came along.
TASHA: Dolores, can I ask? Carmine's watch. Do you still have it?
MRS. ESPOSITO: In my nightstand. It stopped in two thousand three. I never fixed it. It says four seventeen. Forever.
TOMMY: Marcus can fix it. He fixes everything with gears.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Maybe. Someday. Not yet. I like that it stopped. It's honest.
GINA: Dolores. Your last stoop sale. Why? You love it.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I love it. That's why I'm stopping while I love it. I'm seventy-six. My knees count the steps now. Like Eddie's.
EDDIE: Welcome to the club. Forty-two steps. Then you rest.
MRS. ESPOSITO: And someone younger should run the block. Someone with a phone that isn't laminated.
TASHA: Who? Marisol? She's already running the building.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Not Marisol. She's busy enough. I'm thinking of someone else. Someone who knows every name. And every price. And every rule.
GINA: Who?
MRS. ESPOSITO: I'll tell you on Saturday. After the sale. If he says yes.
TOMMY: "He"? Who's "he"? Dolores, tell me.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Saturday, Tommy. Patience. You'll be the MC. You'll be the first to know.
TOMMY: Okay. Then my answer is yes. I'll be the MC. With a loud voice. And a lemon.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Thank you. Now I'm going home. Before I say something kind again. It's exhausting.

[Scene: 10:10 p.m. The bar, after Mrs. Esposito has gone home. Tommy, Gina, Eddie, and Tasha sit together at one end.]

TASHA: Okay. Who is "he"? The new block president? I need to know. I can't sleep without gossip.
EDDIE: Not me. I'm eighty-four. I have one job. Knocking on the floor.
TASHA: Walter? He just moved in today. That's fast.
GINA: Marcus? He knows every delivery worker in Red Hook.
TOMMY: Marcus has a new employee and a city contract. He has no time.
TASHA: Jesse? He doesn't even live here yet.
EDDIE: "Someone who knows every name. And every price. And every rule." Think. Who counts everything? Who ran forty-one times at the pantry?
GINA: Oh no. Diego? He's ten!
EDDIE: He's eleven in February. And he's the runner. And the future treasurer.
TOMMY: A ten-year-old block association president? Is that even legal?
TASHA: Is anything on this block legal?
GINA: Dad. Can I say something? About the lemons. You never told me either.
TOMMY: You were in Carroll Gardens. With Luca. He was a baby. You had enough.
GINA: Dad. No secrets. Rule from the napkin.
TOMMY: The napkin rule started in October. The lemons were twenty twenty. Old secrets don't count.
GINA: Old secrets count double. Mom's rule.
TOMMY: That's not your mother's rule.
GINA: It is now. I just made it up. Like last time.
EDDIE: She's getting good at that, Tommy. Making up your wife's rules.
TOMMY: She learned from the best. Angela made up rules all the time. "No hats at the bar." "No sad songs before nine." "One lemon in every bag."
TASHA: "One lemon in every bag." That's a good rule. I'm stealing it. For my nursing bag.

[Scene: 10:30 p.m. The second-floor landing. Mrs. Esposito unlocks the door of 2B. Tommy comes up the stairs behind her with a small paper bag.]

TOMMY: Dolores. Wait. One second.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Tommy? What is it? It's late.
TOMMY: Here. From the bar. Eggs. Milk. Bread. Cat food. And one lemon.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Oh, Tommy. You didn't have to.
TOMMY: I know. That's why I did. And this time, I'm not running down the stairs. I'm standing right here. So you can see me.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I see you. I've always seen you. Even with the mask.
TOMMY: Dolores, can I ask you? Who's the new president? The "he"? Eddie thinks it's Diego.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Eddie thinks too much. For a man who sleeps at nine.
TOMMY: So it's not Diego?
MRS. ESPOSITO: I didn't say that. I said Saturday.
TOMMY: You're enjoying this.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Tommy, I'm seventy-six. Secrets are my last hobby. Let me have one. A good one.
TOMMY: You never said anything. Seven years.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Some things you say in letters. Not on stairs. Stairs are for knocking.
TOMMY: Then knock on my door sometimes. Not only Eddie's ceiling. I'm on the second floor too. Right across the hall.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Two knocks?
TOMMY: Two knocks. I'll knock back. Like Eddie.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Then I'll need a bigger system. A second card. Laminated.
TOMMY: Of course you will. Goodnight, Dolores.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Goodnight, Tommy. Thank you for the lemon. Carmine would have liked you. He was loud too.
TOMMY: What did Carmine do? You never said.
MRS. ESPOSITO: He drove a bus. The B61. For eighteen years.
TOMMY: The B61? Like Walter?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Like Walter. That's why I fixed his appeal so fast. I saw him in the pantry line in his old MTA jacket. And I thought, "Carmine would want me to help this man."
TOMMY: Dolores. You've been taking care of this whole neighborhood. Quietly. For thirty years.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Not quietly, Tommy. With a clipboard. Very loudly. Goodnight.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **편지체 표현**
  - *Dear Tommy, I am writing to say…* — "~을 말하려고 이 편지를 씁니다." 편지의 정석 시작.
  - *I owe you an apology.* — "당신에게 사과할 것이 있습니다."
  - *I was wrong to compare you to Richie.* — "당신을 리치에게 비교한 것은 잘못이었습니다." *I was wrong to + 동사*.
  - *I should have said that first.* — "그 말을 먼저 했어야 했습니다."
  - *I will never forget those bags.* — "그 장바구니들을 절대 잊지 못할 겁니다."
  - *Please accept my apology.* — "제 사과를 받아 주세요."
  - *With gratitude, and with respect. Yours truly,* — 감사와 존경을 담은 맺음말.
  - *P.S.* — 추신. 부인의 편지에는 늘 있다.
- **The clipboard was my armor.** — "클립보드는 내 갑옷이었다."
- **That is not corruption. That is love.** — "그건 부패가 아니라 사랑이다."
- **Pay me when you can.** — 살이 1995년에 한 말. "낼 수 있을 때 내요."
- **I'm stopping while I love it.** — "좋아할 때 그만두는 거야."
- **Stairs are for knocking.** — "계단은 두드리라고 있는 거야."
- **master of ceremonies (MC)** — 행사 사회자.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- 편지는 지나가 대신 읽는다. 편지체 낭독 톤: 대화보다 느리고, 문장 끝을 분명히 내린다. 따옴표 안의 다른 사람 말(*'The lease is not yours.'*)은 목소리를 살짝 바꾼다.
- 단락이 바뀔 때(지나의 다음 대사로 넘어갈 때) 숨을 한 번 쉰다.
- *"Always one lemon."*에서 멈춘다. 이 화의 열쇠다.
- 토미의 *...You knew?*는 작게, 놀라서.
- 부인은 감정을 숨기려고 건조하게 말한다. 마지막 *Very loudly. | Goodnight.*에서 미소가 느껴지게.

## 한글 해석

에스포지토 부인: 지나. 부탁 하나 해도 돼요? 좀 이상한 부탁이요?
지나: 물론이죠, 돌로레스. 뭐든지요.
에스포지토 부인: 이건 편지예요. 아버지께 드리는 거예요. 타자로 쳤어요. 내 타자기로요. 초안을 세 번 썼어요.
지나: 아빠한테요? 왜 직접 안 주세요? 바로 저기 계신데요.
에스포지토 부인: 소리 내서 못 읽겠어서요. 목소리가 떨릴 거예요. 그러다 멈추고. 그다음엔 그걸 감추려고 날카로운 말을 하겠죠. 내가 늘 그래요.
지나: 제가 읽어 드리길 원하세요? 아빠한테요?
에스포지토 부인: 네. 부탁해요. 소리 내서요. 여기서요. 난 에디 옆에 앉을게요. 들을게요. 그리고 끼어들지 않을게요. 이번만은요.
에디: 그건 보고 싶네. 돌로레스가 끼어들지 않는 거.
에스포지토 부인: 조용히 해요, 에디. 연습 중이에요.
에디: 뭐라고 썼어? 말해 줄 수 있어? 미리 보기로?
에스포지토 부인: 미리 보기 없어요. 다 같이 들어요.
에디: 내 얘기야?
에스포지토 부인: 모든 게 에디 얘기는 아니에요. 몇 가지만요.
지나: 아빠. 이리 와 보실래요? 돌로레스가 아빠한테 편지를 쓰셨어요. 제가 읽어 드리래요.
토미: 편지? 나한테? 나 혼나는 거야? 비계 얘기야?
에스포지토 부인: 비계 얘기 아니에요.
타샤: 저 나갈까요? 사적인 얘기 같은데요.
에스포지토 부인: 아니, 타샤. 있어. 내가 회의에서 한 말은 다들 들었잖아. 이것도 다들 들어야 해.
토미: 알았어. 알았어. 앉았다. 읽어, 지나.
지나: "토미에게. 이사회에서 하지 못한 말을 하려고 이 편지를 씁니다. 당신에게 사과할 것이 있습니다."
지나: "열흘 전, 당신은 이사회에 3B를 지나와 루카에게 주자고 했습니다. 나는 그것이 리치가 한 짓처럼 보일 거라고 했습니다. 가족 먼저. 규칙은 나중."
지나: "그렇게 말한 것은 잘못이었습니다. 규칙에 대해선 틀리지 않았습니다. 규칙은 옳았습니다. 하지만 당신을 리치에게 비교한 것은 잘못이었습니다. 당신은 리치와 전혀 다릅니다."
토미: …
지나: "리치는 자기 빚을 갚으려고 이 건물을 낯선 사람들에게 팔았습니다. 당신은 가진 모든 것을 걸고 이 건물을 위해 싸웠습니다. 아무것도 없을 때조차요. 당신은 딸에게 집을 주고 싶었습니다. 그건 부패가 아닙니다. 그건 사랑입니다. 그 말을 먼저 했어야 했습니다."
토미: 돌로레스…
에스포지토 부인: 쉿. 끝까지 읽게 해요. 더 있어요. 두 장이에요.
지나: "내가 왜 그렇게 규칙에 집착하는지 궁금할지도 모르겠습니다. 왜 클립보드를 갖고 다니는지. 왜 모든 걸 세는지. 이 건물의 누구에게도 말한 적이 없습니다. 에디에게조차도요."
에디: 안 했어. 한 번 물어본 적 있지. 2005년에. 쓰레기 수거 일정으로 화제를 바꾸더라고.
지나: "1994년에 남편 카르미네가 세상을 떠났습니다. 아주 갑자기요. 심장마비였습니다. 지하철에서요. 퇴근길에요. 그는 쉰한 살이었습니다."
타샤: 아, 돌로레스. 몰랐어요.
지나: "우리는 벤슨허스트에 집이 있었습니다. 그의 이름으로요. 그의 이름으로만요. 그가 죽은 뒤 집주인은 내게 30일을 주었습니다. '계약은 당신 것이 아닙니다'라고 했습니다. 나는 MTA에 직장이 있었습니다. 하지만 집이 없었습니다. 마흔네 살이었습니다."
지나: "성당의 한 여성이 레드훅의 건물 하나를 알려 주었습니다. 살이라는 집주인이요. 나는 빗속에서 이곳에 왔습니다. 1995년 3월에요. 가방 하나와 카르미네의 시계를 들고요."
