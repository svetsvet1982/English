# THE CORNER HOUSE — 1부 14화

## Mother's Day Brunch (어머니날 브런치)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 1부 14화 · 시기: 2026년 5월 10일(일), 어머니날 · 난이도: A2~B1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 선셋파크, 마리솔 부모님의 아파트 (현관 → 부엌 → 식탁)
> - 등장인물: MARISOL, DIEGO, CARMEN, ARTURO, HÉCTOR
> - 사건: 어머니날 브런치. 외식은 너무 비싸서 결국 엄마 카르멘이 요리한다. 누가 요리하느냐, 누구 타말레가 더 맛있느냐로 실랑이하다가 아르투로가 이민 이야기를 꺼낸다.
> - 학습 포인트: 가족 모임 화법, 선호 표현(would rather, prefer, like A better than B), 칭찬
> - 미국 현안: 이민 1세대 가족, 외식 물가, 이민 단속 뉴스로 인한 불안(가볍게)
> - 연결: 아르투로는 늘 영주권을 지갑에 넣고 다님 → 61화. 금요일마다 디에고가 조부모 댁에서 타말레를 배우기로 함. 남동생 엑토르 첫 등장.

## 대본

[Scene: Sunday, 11:00 a.m. An apartment in Sunset Park. The door opens.]

DIEGO: Abuela! Feliz Día de las Madres!
CARMEN: Ay, mi amor! Come here. Let me look at you. You're so tall!
DIEGO: I grew two inches. Mom measured.
CARMEN: Two inches! What are you eating? Rocks?
DIEGO: Pizza, mostly.
MARISOL: Hi, Mami. Happy Mother's Day.
CARMEN: Mija. Happy Mother's Day to you too. You're a mother also.
MARISOL: These are for you. Tulips. Your favorite.
CARMEN: Yellow tulips! Beautiful. You remembered.
MARISOL: You say it every year. "Yellow tulips, not red roses."
CARMEN: Red roses are for funerals. Yellow is for happiness.
ARTURO: Is that my grandson? Is that Diego Reyes?
DIEGO: Abuelo! Hi!
ARTURO: Look at these muscles. Are you playing soccer?
DIEGO: Every Saturday. And I fix bikes with Marcus.
ARTURO: You fix bikes? Like a mechanic? Good. Good hands are important.
MARISOL: Hi, Papi. Something smells amazing. What's cooking?
CARMEN: Chilaquiles. Eggs. Beans. Tamales from Friday.
MARISOL: Mami, no! It's Mother's Day. You're not supposed to cook today.
CARMEN: Who else is going to cook? Your father?
ARTURO: I can cook. I cooked in restaurants for thirty years.
CARMEN: In restaurants, yes. At home, never.
ARTURO: At home, you never let me.
MARISOL: That's why I said let's go out. To a restaurant. My treat.
CARMEN: A restaurant? For brunch? Do you know how much brunch costs?
MARISOL: I know. It's expensive. But it's one day.
CARMEN: Your cousin went to a brunch in Manhattan. Sixty dollars a person. For eggs!
ARTURO: Sixty dollars? For eggs? I made eggs for a living. Nobody paid me sixty dollars.
DIEGO: Eggs are expensive now, Abuelo. Seven dollars a dozen.
ARTURO: Seven! In nineteen eighty-seven, a dozen was eighty cents.
CARMEN: So, no restaurant. We eat here. I cook. Everybody's happy.
MARISOL: Then at least let me help. I'll make the coffee.
CARMEN: Okay. You make the coffee. But not too strong. Your coffee is always too strong.
MARISOL: My coffee is perfect.
ARTURO: Her coffee is better than mine. I prefer hers.

[Scene: The kitchen. Pots on every burner.]

CARMEN: Marisol, taste this. Does it need more salt?
MARISOL: Mm. No. It's perfect. Maybe a little more lime.
CARMEN: Lime? In the salsa verde? You sound like your father.
MARISOL: Papi is right sometimes.
CARMEN: Sometimes. Once a year. Today is not that day.
MARISOL: Where's Héctor? He said he'd be here at eleven.
CARMEN: Your brother is always late. Since he was born. Two weeks late!
HÉCTOR: I'm here! I'm here! Happy Mother's Day, Ma!
CARMEN: Héctor! You're late. Where are your wife and the girls?
HÉCTOR: Sofia's with her mother in Queens. We'll switch at four. Two Mother's Days. Very complicated. Here. I brought flowers.
CARMEN: Thank you, mijo. From the bodega?
HÉCTOR: From the... flower shop.
MARISOL: There's still the bodega price sticker on it. Nine ninety-nine.
HÉCTOR: Thanks, sis. Very helpful.
CARMEN: It doesn't matter. Flowers are flowers. Put them in water.
HÉCTOR: How's my favorite sister?
MARISOL: Your only sister is tired. But good.
HÉCTOR: Tired? From what? You work from home.
MARISOL: On Mondays. The other days, I'm in the office. Then pickup, homework, dinner, laundry. Alone.
HÉCTOR: Okay, okay. Sorry. You're a superhero.
MARISOL: I'm not a superhero. I'm just a mom with no time.
CARMEN: That's what a superhero is, mija.
HÉCTOR: Ma, can I have a tamal? I'm starving.
CARMEN: Wait for the table. Like a gentleman.
HÉCTOR: Which do you like better, Marisol? Ma's tamales or Tía Rosa's?
MARISOL: Don't make me choose. That's a dangerous question.
CARMEN: Choose.
MARISOL: Yours, Mami. Of course. Yours are softer. Tía Rosa's are drier.
CARMEN: Good answer. You can have two.
HÉCTOR: What about me? I prefer yours too!
CARMEN: You came late. You get one.
ARTURO: The table is ready! Everybody sit!
DIEGO: I set the table! I folded the napkins like birds!
HÉCTOR: Like birds? Those look like... frogs.
DIEGO: They're birds! Abuela, tell him!
CARMEN: They're beautiful birds, mi amor. Héctor, be nice.
HÉCTOR: Very beautiful birds. My mistake.
ARTURO: Okay. Before we eat, a toast. To the mothers.
HÉCTOR: To the mothers!

[Scene: Around the table. Coffee cups raised.]

ARTURO: To Carmen. Forty-two years. She came with me from Puebla with one suitcase.
CARMEN: One small suitcase. And you.
ARTURO: And me. The heavy part.
DIEGO: Abuelo, why did you come to New York?
ARTURO: Work. In Puebla, there was no work for me. My cousin was here. In a restaurant in Manhattan.
DIEGO: Was it hard?
ARTURO: Very hard. I washed dishes for two years. Then I cooked. Then I was the head cook.
CARMEN: And then we opened our taco shop. On Fifth Avenue. In Sunset Park. Not the fancy Fifth Avenue.
HÉCTOR: Reyes Tacos. The best tacos in Brooklyn. For twenty-two years.
MARISOL: I did my homework in that shop. Every day. Between the tortillas.
DIEGO: Why did you close it?
ARTURO: We got old. And the rent went up. Like everything.
CARMEN: Now a coffee shop is there. Eight dollars for a latte.
MARISOL: And they don't even have horchata.
ARTURO: Eight dollars. With that money, I could make a whole lunch.
DIEGO: Abuelo, do you like it here? In America?
ARTURO: I love it here. My children are here. My grandson is here. This is home.
CARMEN: But your father still carries his papers. Every day. In his wallet.
ARTURO: My green card. Always. Since the news got scary.
MARISOL: Papi, you're a legal resident. For thirty years. You don't have to worry.
ARTURO: I know, mija. But I'd rather be safe. Old habits.
HÉCTOR: Okay. No serious talk today. It's Mother's Day.
DIEGO: Abuela, I made you a card. Open it.
CARMEN: Ay, look at this! Is this me? With a crown?
DIEGO: You're the queen of tamales.
CARMEN: The queen of tamales! I'll put it on the wall. Next to the Virgin.
DIEGO: And I made one for Mom too. But it's a surprise. For later.
MARISOL: For me? Thank you, baby.
CARMEN: Marisol, listen to me. You're a good mother. A very good mother.
MARISOL: Mami, stop. You'll make me cry.
CARMEN: Cry. It's Mother's Day. Crying is allowed.
ARTURO: When you need help, call us. We're retired. We have time.
MARISOL: You have doctor's appointments every week.
ARTURO: Doctor's appointments, and time. Both.
HÉCTOR: Bring Diego on Fridays. Ma would love that.
CARMEN: I'd love that. I'd rather have Diego than the TV.
DIEGO: Can we make tamales on Fridays?
CARMEN: Every Friday. I'll teach you. Like I taught your mother.
MARISOL: Thank you, Mami. Really. That would help a lot.
ARTURO: Okay. Now, music. Héctor, the speaker.
HÉCTOR: What do you want, Pa? Cumbia? Mariachi?
ARTURO: Something to dance to. I want to dance with my wife.
CARMEN: Arturo, I'm cooking. My hands are dirty.
ARTURO: It's Mother's Day. Today, you don't cook. Today, you dance.
DIEGO: Mom, come on! Dance with me!
MARISOL: Okay, okay. But nobody films this. Héctor, put your phone away!

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **Feliz Día de las Madres / mija·mijo / mi amor / Ay** — 스페인어 섞기. *mija*(우리 딸), *mijo*(우리 아들). 이민 가정에서는 영어 문장 속에 이런 호칭이 자연스럽게 섞인다.
- **You're not supposed to cook today.** — "오늘은 요리하면 안 되잖아." be supposed to는 '원래 ~하기로 되어 있다'.
- **My treat.** — "내가 낼게."
- **for a living** — 생계로. (I made eggs for a living.)
- **선호 표현** — *I prefer hers.* / *Which do you like better, A or B?* / *I'd rather be safe.* / *I'd rather have Diego than the TV.*
- **Don't make me choose.** — "고르게 하지 마." 곤란한 비교 질문을 피할 때.
- **Good answer.** — "모범 답안이네."
- **칭찬** — *You're so tall! / Beautiful. / You're a good mother. / The best tacos in Brooklyn.*
- **Like a gentleman.** — "신사답게."
- **My mistake.** — "내가 틀렸네." 가벼운 사과.
- **a toast / To the mothers!** — 건배 / "어머니들을 위하여!"
- **the heavy part** — 무거운 짐. 아르투로의 자기 비하 농담.
- **green card / legal resident** — 영주권 / 합법 영주자.
- **Old habits.** — (Old habits die hard.) "오랜 습관이라서."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **스페인어 단어는 스페인어 발음으로**: *mija* [미하], *Héctor* [엑토르], *abuela* [아부엘라], *chilaquiles* [칠라킬레스], *tamales* [타말레스]. 영어 원어민 가족도 이 단어들은 그대로 발음한다. 영어 부분은 미국식으로. 이 전환의 리듬이 장면의 핵심.
- **I'd rather** [아이드 래더r] — 'd를 흘리지 말고. **I'd love that** [아이들러v 댓].
- **Puebla** [푸에블라], **Sunset Park** [썬셋 파r크] — 강세 SUN-set.
- **nine ninety-nine** — 가격표 숫자. 9달러 99센트.
- 건배 장면 "To the mothers!"는 다 같이 높게. 아르투로의 이민 이야기는 천천히, 담담하게.
- 카르멘의 "Choose."는 한 단어로 툭, 장난스럽지만 단호하게.
