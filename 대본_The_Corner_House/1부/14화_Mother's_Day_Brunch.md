# THE CORNER HOUSE — 1부 14화

## Mother's Day Brunch (어머니날 브런치)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 1부 14화 · 시기: 2026년 5월 10일(일), 어머니날 · 난이도: A2~B1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 선셋파크, 마리솔 부모님의 아파트 (현관 → 부엌 → 식탁)
> - 등장인물: MARISOL, DIEGO, CARMEN, ARTURO, HÉCTOR
> - 사건: 어머니날 브런치. 외식은 너무 비싸서 결국 엄마 카르멘이 요리한다. 누가 요리하느냐, 누구 타말레가 더 맛있느냐로 실랑이하다가 아르투로가 이민 이야기를 꺼낸다.
> - 학습 포인트: 가족 모임 화법, 선호 표현(would rather, prefer, like A better than B), 칭찬
> - 미국 현안: 이민 1세대 가족, 외식 물가, 이민 단속 뉴스로 인한 불안(가볍게)
> - 연결: 아르투로는 늘 영주권을 지갑에 넣고 다님 → 61화. 금요일마다 디에고가 조부모 댁에서 타말레를 배우기로 함. 남동생 엑토르 첫 등장.

## 대본

[Scene: Sunday, 11:00 a.m. An apartment in Sunset Park. The door opens.]

DIEGO: Abuela! Feliz Día de las Madres!
CARMEN: Ay, mi amor! Come here and let me look at you. You're so tall!
DIEGO: I grew two inches! Mom measured me against the door.
CARMEN: Two inches! What are you eating over there? Rocks?
DIEGO: Pizza, mostly.
MARISOL: Hi, Mami. Happy Mother's Day.
CARMEN: Mija. Happy Mother's Day to you too, because you're a mother also.
MARISOL: These are for you. They're tulips, your favorite.
CARMEN: Yellow tulips! Beautiful. You remembered, mija.
MARISOL: You say it every single year. "Yellow tulips, not red roses."
CARMEN: Red roses are for funerals. Yellow is for happiness.
ARTURO: Is that my grandson? Is that really Diego Reyes?
DIEGO: Abuelo! Hi!
ARTURO: Look at these muscles. Are you still playing soccer?
DIEGO: Every Saturday. And I fix bikes with Marcus at his shop.
ARTURO: You fix bikes? Like a real mechanic? Good. Good hands are important.
MARISOL: Hi, Papi. Something smells amazing in here. What's cooking?
CARMEN: Chilaquiles, eggs, and beans. And there are tamales from Friday.
MARISOL: Mami, no! It's Mother's Day. You're not supposed to cook today.
CARMEN: Who else is going to cook? Your father?
ARTURO: Hey, I can cook. I cooked in restaurants for thirty years.
CARMEN: In restaurants, yes. But at home, never.
ARTURO: At home, you never let me, Carmen.
MARISOL: That's why I said let's go out to a restaurant. My treat.
CARMEN: A restaurant, for brunch? Do you know how much brunch costs?
MARISOL: I know it's expensive. But it's only one day a year.
CARMEN: Your cousin went to a brunch in Manhattan. It was sixty dollars a person, for eggs!
ARTURO: Sixty dollars for eggs? I made eggs for a living, and nobody paid me sixty dollars.
DIEGO: Eggs are expensive now, Abuelo. They're seven dollars a dozen.
ARTURO: Seven! In nineteen eighty-seven, a dozen was eighty cents.
CARMEN: So, no restaurant. We eat here, I cook, and everybody's happy.
MARISOL: Then at least let me help you. I'll make the coffee.
CARMEN: Okay, you make the coffee. But not too strong, because your coffee is always too strong.
MARISOL: My coffee is perfect, Mami.
ARTURO: Her coffee is better than mine. Honestly, I prefer hers.

[Scene: The kitchen. Pots on every burner.]

CARMEN: Marisol, taste this. Does it need more salt?
MARISOL: Mm. No, it's perfect. Maybe it needs a little more lime.
CARMEN: Lime? In the salsa verde? You sound just like your father.
MARISOL: Well, Papi is right sometimes.
CARMEN: Sometimes, maybe once a year. And today is not that day.
MARISOL: Where's Héctor? He said he'd be here at eleven.
CARMEN: Your brother is always late, since the day he was born. He was two weeks late!
HÉCTOR: I'm here! I'm here! Happy Mother's Day, Ma!
CARMEN: Héctor! You're late again. Where are your wife and the girls?
HÉCTOR: Sofia's with her mother in Queens, and we'll switch at four. Two Mother's Days is very complicated. Here, I brought you flowers.
CARMEN: Thank you, mijo. Did you get them from the bodega?
HÉCTOR: I got them from the... flower shop.
MARISOL: The bodega price sticker is still on it. It says nine ninety-nine.
HÉCTOR: Thanks, sis. That's very helpful.
CARMEN: It doesn't matter. Flowers are flowers, so put them in water.
HÉCTOR: So how's my favorite sister doing?
MARISOL: Your only sister is tired. But she's good.
HÉCTOR: Tired? From what? You work from home.
MARISOL: Only on Mondays. The other days, I'm in the office. Then it's pickup, homework, dinner, and laundry, all alone.
HÉCTOR: Okay, okay, I'm sorry. You're a superhero.
MARISOL: I'm not a superhero. I'm just a mom with no time.
CARMEN: That's exactly what a superhero is, mija.
HÉCTOR: Ma, can I have a tamal now? I'm starving.
CARMEN: Wait for the table, like a gentleman.
HÉCTOR: Which do you like better, Marisol? Ma's tamales or Tía Rosa's?
MARISOL: Don't make me choose. That's a dangerous question in this family.
CARMEN: Choose.
MARISOL: Yours, Mami, of course. Yours are softer, and Tía Rosa's are a little drier.
CARMEN: Good answer. You can have two.
HÉCTOR: What about me? I prefer yours too, Ma!
CARMEN: You came late, so you get one.
ARTURO: The table is ready! Everybody, come and sit down!
DIEGO: I set the table! And I folded the napkins like birds!
HÉCTOR: Like birds? Those look more like... frogs.
DIEGO: They're birds! Abuela, tell him they're birds!
CARMEN: They're beautiful birds, mi amor. Héctor, be nice to him.
HÉCTOR: They're very beautiful birds. My mistake.
ARTURO: Okay, before we eat, let's have a toast. To the mothers.
HÉCTOR: To the mothers!

[Scene: Around the table. Coffee cups raised.]

ARTURO: To Carmen, after forty-two years. She came with me from Puebla with one suitcase.
CARMEN: It was one small suitcase. And you.
ARTURO: And me, the heavy part.
DIEGO: Abuelo, why did you come to New York?
ARTURO: For work. In Puebla, there was no work for me. My cousin was here, at a restaurant in Manhattan.
DIEGO: Was it hard at the beginning?
ARTURO: It was very hard. I washed dishes for two years. Then I cooked, and then I was the head cook.
CARMEN: And then we opened our taco shop on Fifth Avenue, here in Sunset Park. Not the fancy Fifth Avenue.
HÉCTOR: Reyes Tacos, the best tacos in Brooklyn. For twenty-two years.
MARISOL: I did my homework in that shop every day, between the tortillas.
DIEGO: So why did you close it?
ARTURO: We got old. And the rent went up, just like everything else.
CARMEN: Now there's a coffee shop there. They charge eight dollars for a latte.
MARISOL: And they don't even have horchata.
ARTURO: Eight dollars! With that money, I could make a whole lunch.
DIEGO: Abuelo, do you like it here in America?
ARTURO: I love it here. My children are here, and my grandson is here. This is home.
CARMEN: But your grandfather still carries his papers every day, in his wallet.
ARTURO: My green card, always. I started when the news got scary.
MARISOL: Papi, you've been a legal resident for thirty years. You don't have to worry.
ARTURO: I know, mija. But I'd rather be safe. Old habits.
HÉCTOR: Okay, no serious talk today. It's Mother's Day.
DIEGO: Abuela, I made you a card. Open it!
CARMEN: Ay, look at this! Is this me? With a crown on my head?
DIEGO: Yes, because you're the queen of tamales.
CARMEN: The queen of tamales! I'll put it on the wall, next to the Virgin.
DIEGO: And I made one for Mom too. But it's a surprise for later.
MARISOL: For me? Thank you, baby.
CARMEN: Marisol, listen to me. You're a good mother, a very good mother.
MARISOL: Mami, stop. You're going to make me cry.
CARMEN: So cry. It's Mother's Day, and crying is allowed.
ARTURO: When you need help, call us. We're retired, so we have time.
MARISOL: But you have doctor's appointments every week.
ARTURO: We have doctor's appointments, and we have time. Both.
HÉCTOR: Bring Diego here on Fridays. Ma would love that.
CARMEN: I'd love that. I'd rather have Diego than the TV.
DIEGO: Can we make tamales together on Fridays?
CARMEN: Every Friday. I'll teach you, like I taught your mother.
MARISOL: Thank you, Mami. Really, that would help me a lot.
ARTURO: Okay, now it's time for music. Héctor, get the speaker.
HÉCTOR: What do you want, Pa? Cumbia? Mariachi?
ARTURO: Something to dance to, because I want to dance with my wife.
CARMEN: Arturo, I'm cooking. My hands are dirty.
ARTURO: It's Mother's Day. Today, you don't cook. Today, you dance.
DIEGO: Mom, come on! Come dance with me!
MARISOL: Okay, okay. But nobody films this. Héctor, put your phone away!

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **Feliz Día de las Madres / mija·mijo / mi amor / Ay** — 스페인어 섞기. *mija*(우리 딸), *mijo*(우리 아들). 이민 가정에서는 영어 문장 속에 이런 호칭이 자연스럽게 섞인다.
- **You're not supposed to cook today.** — "오늘은 요리하면 안 되잖아." be supposed to는 '원래 ~하기로 되어 있다'.
- **My treat.** — "내가 낼게."
- **for a living** — 생계로. (I made eggs for a living, and nobody paid me sixty dollars.)
- **선호 표현** — *I prefer hers.* / *Which do you like better, A or B?* / *I'd rather be safe.* / *I'd rather have Diego than the TV.*
- **Don't make me choose.** — "고르게 하지 마." 곤란한 비교 질문을 피할 때.
- **Good answer.** — "모범 답안이네."
- **칭찬** — *You're so tall! / Beautiful. / You're a good mother. / Reyes Tacos, the best tacos in Brooklyn.*
- **Like a gentleman.** — "신사답게."
- **My mistake.** — "내가 틀렸네." 가벼운 사과.
- **a toast / To the mothers!** — 건배 / "어머니들을 위하여!"
- **the heavy part** — 무거운 짐. 아르투로의 자기 비하 농담.
- **green card / legal resident** — 영주권 / 합법 영주자.
- **Old habits.** — (Old habits die hard.) "오랜 습관이라서."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **스페인어 단어는 스페인어 발음으로**: *mija* [미하], *Héctor* [엑토르], *abuela* [아부엘라], *chilaquiles* [칠라킬레스], *tamales* [타말레스]. 영어 원어민 가족도 이 단어들은 그대로 발음한다. 영어 부분은 미국식으로. 이 전환의 리듬이 장면의 핵심.
- **I'd rather** [아이드 래더r] — 'd를 흘리지 말고. **I'd love that** [아이들러v 댓].
- **Puebla** [푸에블라], **Sunset Park** [썬셋 파r크] — 강세 SUN-set.
- **nine ninety-nine** — 가격표 숫자. 9달러 99센트.
- 건배 장면 "To the mothers!"는 다 같이 높게. 아르투로의 이민 이야기는 천천히, 담담하게.
- 카르멘의 "Choose."는 한 단어로 툭, 장난스럽지만 단호하게.

디에고: 처음엔 힘들었어요?
아르투로: 아주 힘들었지. 2년 동안 설거지를 했어. 그다음 요리를 했고, 그다음 주방장이 됐지.
카르멘: 그리고 여기 선셋파크 5번가에 우리 타코 가게를 열었지. 그 비싼 5번가 말고.
엑토르: 레예스 타코, 브루클린 최고의 타코. 22년 동안.
마리솔: 난 그 가게에서 매일 숙제했어요, 토르티야 사이에서.
디에고: 그럼 왜 문 닫았어요?
아르투로: 우리가 늙었지. 그리고 다른 모든 것처럼 월세가 올랐어.
카르멘: 지금은 거기 커피숍이 있어. 라테 한 잔에 8달러 받아.
마리솔: 그리고 오르차타도 안 팔아요.
아르투로: 8달러! 그 돈이면 점심 한 끼를 다 차릴 수 있는데.
디에고: 할아버지, 여기 미국이 좋아요?
아르투로: 아주 좋지. 내 자식들이 여기 있고, 내 손자가 여기 있어. 여기가 집이야.
카르멘: 근데 네 할아버지는 아직도 매일 서류를 지갑에 넣고 다녀.
아르투로: 내 영주권, 늘. 뉴스가 무서워졌을 때부터 그랬어.
마리솔: 아빠, 30년째 합법 영주자잖아요. 걱정 안 하셔도 돼요.
아르투로: 알아, 딸아. 그래도 조심하는 게 낫지. 오랜 습관이야.
엑토르: 자, 오늘은 심각한 얘기 금지. 어머니날이잖아요.
디에고: 할머니, 카드 만들었어요. 열어 보세요!
카르멘: 아이고, 이것 좀 봐! 이게 나야? 머리에 왕관 쓰고?
디에고: 네, 할머니는 타말레의 여왕이니까요.
카르멘: 타말레의 여왕! 벽에 붙여 놔야지, 성모님 옆에.
디에고: 그리고 엄마 것도 만들었어요. 근데 이따 줄 비밀이에요.
마리솔: 엄마 거? 고마워, 아가.
카르멘: 마리솔, 엄마 말 들어. 넌 좋은 엄마야, 아주 좋은 엄마야.
마리솔: 엄마, 그만해요. 저 울게 생겼어요.
카르멘: 그럼 울어. 어머니날이잖아, 우는 건 허락이야.
아르투로: 도움이 필요하면 전화해. 우린 은퇴했으니까 시간 많아.
마리솔: 그래도 매주 병원 예약 있잖아요.
아르투로: 병원 예약도 있고, 시간도 있지. 둘 다.
엑토르: 금요일마다 디에고 여기 데려와. 엄마가 엄청 좋아하실 거야.
카르멘: 아주 좋지. TV보다 디에고가 훨씬 낫지.
디에고: 금요일마다 같이 타말레 만들어도 돼요?
카르멘: 금요일마다. 가르쳐 줄게, 네 엄마 가르쳤던 것처럼.
마리솔: 고마워요, 엄마. 정말로요, 그럼 저한테 엄청 도움이 될 거예요.
아르투로: 좋아, 이제 음악 시간이다. 엑토르, 스피커 가져와.
엑토르: 뭐 틀까요, 아빠? 쿰비아? 마리아치?
아르투로: 춤출 수 있는 거, 아내랑 춤추고 싶으니까.
카르멘: 아르투로, 나 요리 중이야. 손이 더러워.
아르투로: 어머니날이잖아. 오늘은 요리 안 해. 오늘은 춤춰.
디에고: 엄마, 빨리! 와서 나랑 춤춰요!
마리솔: 알았어, 알았어. 근데 아무도 찍지 마. 엑토르, 휴대폰 치워!
