# THE CORNER HOUSE — 1부 11화

## Eddie Gets a Smartphone (에디, 스마트폰을 갖다)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 1부 11화 · 시기: 2026년 4월, 토요일 오후 · 난이도: A2~B1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 3A 에디의 집 부엌 식탁
> - 등장인물: EDDIE, DIEGO, CALLER(전화 속 목소리), KAYLA(영상통화), MRS. ESPOSITO
> - 사건: 아들이 보낸 스마트폰을 디에고가 설정해 준다. 첫 문자를 보내자마자 '통행료 미납' 사기 문자가 오고, 이어 손녀 목소리를 흉내 낸 AI 사기 전화가 걸려 온다.
> - 학습 포인트: 기술 어휘, 설명·지시 화법, 명령형, 순서 표현(first, second, third, then)
> - 미국 현안: 통행료 미납 사기 문자, AI로 가족 목소리를 복제한 '손주 사칭' 사기
> - 연결: 비밀번호는 1974년 6월 1일 입주일. 에디의 냉장고에 '사기 대처 규칙'이 붙는다. 비스킷 방문 시간(2~5시) 계속.

## 대본

[Scene: Saturday, 1:00 p.m. Apartment 3A. A box on the kitchen table. Diego knocks and comes in.]

DIEGO: Eddie! Mom said the box came! Can I open it?
EDDIE: Come in, kid. It's on the kitchen table. I didn't touch it.
DIEGO: Why not?
EDDIE: I was scared. Phones today are smarter than me.
DIEGO: Don't worry. I'll teach you. It's easy. First, open the box.
EDDIE: You open it. Your fingers are faster.
DIEGO: Okay. Wow. It's brand-new. Look, it's so shiny.
EDDIE: It's so big. My old phone fits in my shirt pocket.
DIEGO: This one fits in your pants pocket. Now hold this button on the side.
EDDIE: Which button? There are three.
DIEGO: The one on the right. Hold it down. Don't let go.
EDDIE: I'm holding it. Nothing's happening.
DIEGO: Wait. Keep holding... There! See? It's turning on.
EDDIE: It says "Hello." In ten languages. Very polite.
DIEGO: Now swipe up. Like this. Put your finger at the bottom and move it up.
EDDIE: Like this?
DIEGO: A little faster. Like you're brushing off a crumb.
EDDIE: There! It moved! What now?
DIEGO: Now choose a language. English. Tap it.
EDDIE: Tap. Not push?
DIEGO: Just tap. Softly. It's not a doorbell.
EDDIE: Okay. Tap. Now it wants Wi-Fi. What's Wi-Fi?
DIEGO: Internet without a wire. Do you have internet, Eddie?
EDDIE: My son pays for something. There's a little box under the TV.
DIEGO: Let me look... Yes. The password is on the box. I'll type it.
EDDIE: Good. My eyes can't read that.
DIEGO: Okay, we're connected. Now you need a passcode. Six numbers.
EDDIE: Six numbers? Why?
DIEGO: So nobody else can open your phone. Choose numbers you remember.
EDDIE: My birthday. Three, one, seven, four, three.
DIEGO: That's five numbers. And everybody knows your birthday. You had a party.
EDDIE: Good point. Okay. The day Peggy and I moved in. Zero, six, zero, one, seven, four.
DIEGO: Perfect. Type it twice. Don't tell me.
EDDIE: You just heard it.
DIEGO: I forgot already. I promise.
EDDIE: Done. Now what? Everything's so small.
DIEGO: We can make the letters bigger. Go to Settings. The gray gear.
EDDIE: The gear. Found it.
DIEGO: Then scroll down to "Display." Scroll means slide your finger up and down.
EDDIE: Scroll. Display. Okay.
DIEGO: Then "Text Size." Move the slider to the right. All the way.
EDDIE: Holy mackerel. Now I can read everything. Even without glasses.
DIEGO: See? Easy. Now let's add your contacts. Who do you call the most?
EDDIE: Michael. Tommy. And... Dolores. For the cat.

[Scene: Twenty minutes later. The contacts are in the phone.]

DIEGO: Okay. Let's send a text to Michael. Open "Messages." The green one.
EDDIE: The green one. Okay. Now where do I write?
DIEGO: Tap the box at the bottom. Then the keyboard comes up.
EDDIE: The letters are tiny. My fingers are big.
DIEGO: Use one finger. Go slowly. Or you can talk. Press the microphone.
EDDIE: I can talk to it? Hello, phone. Tell Michael I have a new phone.
DIEGO: Ha! You don't have to say "Hello, phone." Just say the message.
EDDIE: "Michael, this is Dad. I have the new phone. Diego is helping me."
DIEGO: Good. Now press the blue arrow. That sends it.
EDDIE: Sent! Look at that. My first text message. At eighty-three.
DIEGO: And look. He's typing already. The three dots.
EDDIE: "Welcome to the twenty-first century, Dad." Wise guy.
DIEGO: Now send him a heart. Press here. The little face. Then the heart.
EDDIE: A heart. For my son. Okay. Sent.
DIEGO: Oh, wait. You got another message. From a number.
EDDIE: Who is it? Let me read. "E-ZPass Notice: You have an unpaid toll of four dollars and fifteen cents."
DIEGO: Don't touch it!
EDDIE: Why not? It says I have to pay. "Pay now to avoid a fifty-dollar fine."
DIEGO: It's a scam, Eddie. It's fake. They send it to everybody.
EDDIE: How do you know?
DIEGO: First, do you have a car?
EDDIE: No. I sold my car in two thousand fifteen.
DIEGO: So you don't have any tolls. Second, look at the number. It's not a normal number.
EDDIE: It's very long. Twelve numbers.
DIEGO: Real companies don't text from numbers like that. Third, there's a link. Never click a link like that.
EDDIE: What happens if I click it?
DIEGO: They ask for your credit card. Then they steal your money.
EDDIE: For four dollars and fifteen cents?
DIEGO: No. For everything. Four dollars is just the trick.
EDDIE: Those dirty rats. How did you learn all this?
DIEGO: At school. And my mom gets these every week. She works with lawyers.
EDDIE: So what do I do with it?
DIEGO: Press and hold the message. Then "Report Junk." Then "Delete."
EDDIE: Report Junk. Delete. Gone. Ha! Take that.

[The phone rings. "Unknown Caller" on the screen.]

EDDIE: Now it's ringing. The phone is ringing! What do I press?
DIEGO: Swipe the green button. It says "Unknown." Be careful.
EDDIE: Hello?
CALLER: Grandpa? Grandpa, it's me. It's Kayla.
EDDIE: Kayla? Sweetheart, is that you? You sound strange.
CALLER: I'm crying, Grandpa. I'm in trouble. I had a car accident.
EDDIE: An accident? Are you hurt?
CALLER: I'm okay. But I'm at the police station. I need money for bail. Two thousand dollars.
EDDIE: Two thousand? Of course. I'll call your father.
CALLER: No! Don't call Dad. Please. He'll be so angry. Please, Grandpa. Just you.
DIEGO: Eddie. Eddie. Put it on speaker. Press the button with the sound.
EDDIE: Kayla, how do I send the money?
CALLER: Go to the store. Buy gift cards. Then read me the numbers. Hurry, please.
DIEGO: Gift cards? Eddie, police never want gift cards. It's not her.
EDDIE: It's her voice. It's exactly her voice.
DIEGO: They can copy voices now. With AI. From videos online. Ask her a question.
EDDIE: Kayla, honey. What was Grandma's favorite month?
CALLER: What? Grandpa, there's no time! Please just go to the store!
EDDIE: Tell me. What was Grandma Peggy's favorite month?
CALLER: I... I don't remember. December? Grandpa, please...
EDDIE: Wrong. And you're not my granddaughter. Shame on you.
DIEGO: They hung up! They hung up!
EDDIE: My heart is beating like a drum. Diego, is Kayla okay?
DIEGO: Let's check. Call her real number. From your contacts. Michael sent it.
KAYLA: Hi, Grandpa! Wow, a video call! You have the new phone!
EDDIE: Kayla! It's really you. Are you okay? Are you in jail?
KAYLA: In jail? What? I'm in the library. I'm studying for exams.
EDDIE: Somebody called me. With your voice. Exactly your voice. They wanted two thousand dollars.
KAYLA: Oh my God. That's so scary. Grandpa, did you pay?
EDDIE: No. My teacher here stopped me. Diego, say hello.
DIEGO: Hi, Kayla. It was an AI scam. They wanted gift cards.
KAYLA: Thank you, Diego. You're a hero. Grandpa, if anybody calls like that, hang up. Then call me or Dad.
EDDIE: Hang up. Then call back. I'll remember. I'll write it on the refrigerator.
KAYLA: Love you, Grandpa. I'll tell Dad. Bye, Diego!

[Scene: 2:00 p.m. A knock. Mrs. Esposito with a bag of cat treats. Biscuit is already on the windowsill.]

MRS. ESPOSITO: Eddie? The door was open. It's two o'clock. Biscuit's visiting hours.
EDDIE: Dolores! Come in. Look. I have a smartphone. And I beat a scammer.
MRS. ESPOSITO: You beat a scammer? On your first day?
EDDIE: With Diego's help. They used Kayla's voice. A computer voice.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The same thing happened to my friend Rosemary. She lost five thousand dollars.
DIEGO: Rules, everybody. Never click links. Never pay with gift cards. Hang up and call back.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Write that down for me too, Diego. In big letters.
EDDIE: And make my letters bigger too. All the way to the right.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **명령형으로 설명하기** — *Hold this button. Don't let go. Swipe up. Tap it. Scroll down. Move the slider to the right.* 동사 원형으로 시작하고, 부정은 Don't.
- **순서 표현** — *First, … Second, … Third, … Then …* 디에고는 사기를 알아보는 법을 순서대로 설명한다.
- **기술 어휘** — *turn on*(켜다), *swipe*(밀다), *tap*(톡 누르다), *scroll*(화면을 올리고 내리다), *passcode*(잠금 비밀번호), *Settings*(설정), *contacts*(연락처), *put it on speaker*(스피커폰으로 하다).
- **brush off a crumb** — 부스러기를 털어 내다. 스와이프 동작의 비유.
- **Wise guy.** — "잘난 척하기는." 애정 섞인 핀잔. 에디 세대의 말투.
- **a scam / a scammer** — 사기 / 사기꾼.
- **unpaid toll** — 미납 통행료. 실제로 2024~2025년 미국 전역에 퍼진 사기 문자 유형.
- **Take that.** — "맛 좀 봐라." 상대를 이겼을 때.
- **bail** — 보석금.
- **hang up / call back** — 전화를 끊다 / 다시 걸다.
- **Shame on you.** — 1화에서 에스포지토 부인이 쓴 말을 에디가 사기꾼에게 쓴다.
- **사기 대처 규칙** — *Never click links. Never pay with gift cards. Hang up and call back.* 경찰은 상품권으로 돈을 받지 않는다.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **명령문은 끝을 내린다**: *Tap it.↘ Hold it down.↘ Don't let go.↘* 디에고는 선생님처럼 또박또박.
- **tap / type / swipe** 모음 구별: tap [탭, 입을 옆으로 벌린 æ], type [타입], swipe [스와입].
- **Hold it down** [호울디 다운], **Put it on** [푸리런] — 플랩 T와 연음.
- 숫자를 하나씩 읽을 때: *zero, six, zero, one, seven, four.* 0은 "zero" 또는 "oh"(6화의 seven-oh-five).
- **AI** [에이아이], **E-ZPass** [이지패스].
- 사기 전화 목소리는 급하고 울먹이며, 질문을 피한다. 에디의 "Wrong."은 짧고 단호하게 끊는다.
