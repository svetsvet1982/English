# THE CORNER HOUSE — 4부 71화

## The Last Night Before the Bid (제안 전날 밤)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 4부 17화(통산 71화) · 시기: 2027년 2월 25일(목) 밤 ~ 26일(금) 새벽 · 난이도: B2~C1 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 문 닫은 더 훅 → 같은 곳(회원 표결) → 4A 마리솔의 부엌(제안서 마무리) → 새벽의 바
> - 등장인물: MARISOL, SAM, GINA, NADIA, TOMMY, MRS. ESPOSITO, EDDIE, PRIYA, DIEGO
> - 사건: 최종 최선 제안 마감 전날 밤. 모금 이후 소식이 둘 더 들어왔다. 지나가 신청한 재단이 영상을 보고 3월이 아니라 이번 주에 10만 달러 보조금을 확정했다. 랜드 트러스트 이사회도 땅값 한도를 140만으로 올렸다. 샘은 최종가 335만, 보일러 공제 4만 5천을 빼면 실질 330만 5천을 제시한다. 상대 매수자의 360만보다 30만 가까이 낮다. 대신 빠른 마감, 확정된 자금, 그리고 '사람'을 무기로 삼기로 한다. 다섯 가구가 정족수를 채워 공식 표결을 하고, 만장일치로 가결한다. 밤에는 각 가구의 편지, 살의 편지, 디에고의 그림을 제안서에 첨부한다. 새벽 1시, 토미는 텅 빈 바에서 앤젤라의 카드 앞에 선다.
> - 학습 포인트: 합의·표결 진행(Do we have a quorum? / The motion is to… / All in favor? / Let the record show… / It's unanimous.), 긴장 속의 짧은 발화, 확신과 불안의 표현(I'm confident that… / I'm not sure, but… / It's now or never.), 큰 숫자와 표결 숫자 읽기
> - 미국 현안: 경쟁 입찰에서 가격 외의 강점(자금 확정, 마감 속도, 공동체), 재단 보조금, 주민 자치의 의사 결정
> - 연결: 69화 2/26 정오 봉인 제안, 경쟁자 360만, 3/5 결정, 3/1 시 결정. 70화 모금 113,262+마이클 2천. 67화 재단 두 곳('3월에 연락'). 57화 정족수·가구당 1표. 60화 칼라마리 카드. 37화 살의 편지. 41화 디에고의 그림 실력.

## 대본

[Scene: Thursday, 8:00 p.m. The Hook, closed early. A sign on the door: "CLOSED FOR DEMOCRACY." Sam's laptop is connected to the TV. Marisol, Gina, Nadia, and Tommy sit close to the screen.]

SAM: Okay. Last time. I promise. The final numbers. Then we vote.
TOMMY: You said "last time" on Monday. And Tuesday.
SAM: This time, it's really last time. The bid is due at noon tomorrow. There's no Saturday.
GINA: First, the good news. And there's a lot. Can I go first?
MARISOL: Please. We need good news.
GINA: The foundation. The one that said, "Call us in March." They called me. On Tuesday. They saw Eddie's video. And the article. They moved us up. A hundred thousand dollars. Approved. In writing.
TOMMY: A hundred thousand? Gina! Why didn't you tell me on Tuesday?
GINA: Because I wanted the letter first. It came at four o'clock today. Here. Signed.
NADIA: And I have news too. My board met last night. Emergency meeting. They raised our limit. For the land. One point four million.
MARISOL: One point four! Nadia, that's fifty thousand more.
NADIA: They watched the Community Board video. And Eddie's video. And they read Dolores's list. All forty-seven items. One board member cried. At item thirty-one.
TOMMY: What was thirty-one?
NADIA: "Child changed buckets under leaking roof until four a.m." Diego.
SAM: Okay. So here's where we are. Slide one. The fundraiser: a hundred and fifteen thousand, two hundred sixty-two. With Michael's two thousand. Plus the foundation: a hundred thousand. Plus the land trust's extra fifty.
GINA: That's two hundred sixty-five thousand. More than we needed for three point three.
SAM: Exactly. So the question tonight is simple. How high do we go?
MARISOL: The other buyer offered three point six. We know that.
SAM: We can't reach three point six. If we tried, our monthly fees would go up two hundred dollars a household. Eddie can't pay that. Dolores can't pay that. Nobody should pay that.
NADIA: So we don't try to beat their price. We beat their offer.
TOMMY: What's the difference?
NADIA: Their offer has conditions. Their own inspection. Their own financing. Closing in July. Ours closes in May. With financing that's already approved. Except the city.
GINA: And the city decides on Monday. March first. Before Harborline decides on March fifth.
SAM: So my recommendation. Three point three five. Land, one point four. Building, one point nine five. Minus forty-five for the boiler. Net three point three oh five.
TOMMY: Three point three oh five. Versus three point six. That's three hundred thousand less.
SAM: Two hundred ninety-five thousand less. But with our money already in the bank. And theirs still in a meeting room somewhere.
MARISOL: And the fees? At three point three five?
SAM: Fees go up forty dollars a month per household. From what I showed you in December. Except Eddie. I found a way. His stays at nine fifty.
TOMMY: How?
SAM: The foundation grant has a line for "senior tenant protection." I asked. They said yes.
MARISOL: Sam. You're a hero. A quiet, spreadsheet hero.
SAM: Don't make it weird. Let's vote.

[Scene: 9:00 p.m. The same room. Mrs. Esposito sits at a small table with her clipboard and a pen. Eddie, Sam, and Tommy sit in a row of chairs. Marisol stands. Gina and Nadia watch from the bar.]

MARISOL: Okay. This is an official members' meeting of the Corner House Tenants Association. Dolores, are you ready?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Ready. Minutes are open. Date: February twenty-fifth. Time: nine oh two p.m.
MARISOL: First question. Do we have a quorum?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Five households. Five present. 2A, Mr. Russo. 2B, myself. 3A, Mr. O'Neill. 4A, Ms. Reyes. 4B, Mr. Novak, voting for his household. We have a quorum. We have everybody.
EDDIE: Everybody. That's never happened. Not even at the block party.
MARISOL: Okay. The motion is to submit a best and final offer of three point three five million dollars. With a forty-five-thousand-dollar credit for the boiler. Due tomorrow at noon. Do I hear a motion?
EDDIE: I so move. My first motion. At eighty-three.
TOMMY: I second it. And my hands are sweating.
MARISOL: Is there any discussion? Anybody. Now is the time. If you have a doubt, say it.
SAM: I'll say one thing. As the treasurer. This is the edge. Three point three five is all the money we have. If something breaks in the first year, it's tight. Really tight.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Something always breaks in the first year. In every building. In every marriage.
SAM: Exactly. So I want everybody to know. I'm voting yes. But I'm voting yes with my eyes open. And with one red cell on my spreadsheet.
EDDIE: Then we'll all keep our eyes open. Together. That's the deal.
MRS. ESPOSITO: And for the minutes: what's in the red cell, Sam?
SAM: Roof repairs in year two. Forty thousand. Not funded yet.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Then year two has a fundraiser. Written. "Year two: another Super Bowl."
TOMMY: I have something too. A doubt. A small one.
MARISOL: Go ahead, Tommy.
TOMMY: If we lose... if they pick the other buyer... I don't want anybody to feel like it's their fault. Not Marisol. Not Sam. Nobody. We did everything.
MARISOL: Thank you, Tommy. That goes in the minutes too. Dolores?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Written. "If we lose, it's nobody's fault." Underlined. Twice.
MARISOL: Any more discussion? ...No? Okay. Then let's vote. All in favor?
TOMMY: Aye.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Aye.
EDDIE: Aye. For Peggy. And for Jimmy.
SAM: Aye. For 4B. Priya says aye too. By text. With three hearts.
MARISOL: And 4A votes aye. Any opposed? Any abstentions?
MRS. ESPOSITO: None. Let the record show: the motion carries. Five votes in favor. None opposed. None abstaining. It's unanimous. Nine oh nine p.m.
EDDIE: Unanimous. In Red Hook. Somebody call the newspaper again.
TOMMY: Five for five. Like the Mets in nineteen eighty-six. Game six.
SAM: The Mets lost game six that year, Tommy.
TOMMY: They won game six! Game six was the Buckner game! We won! Sam, you're from Ohio. Don't talk to me about game six.

[Scene: 11:15 p.m. Apartment 4A. The kitchen table is covered with paper. Gina types on a laptop. Priya sorts envelopes. Diego, in pajamas, finishes a drawing with colored pencils. Eddie sits by the window with tea.]

GINA: Okay. The price is in. The financing letters are in. The city application number is in. Now the part that isn't numbers.
PRIYA: The letters. One from each household. I have all five. Dolores typed hers. On the typewriter. It has a postscript.
EDDIE: Of course it does. What does the postscript say?
PRIYA: "P.S. I have a list. Please ask me for it." With her phone number.
GINA: Perfect. And Sal's letter. Teresa sent a clean copy. With his signature. From twenty twenty-three.
EDDIE: Read it once more. Out loud. For the room.
GINA: "To my children. If you ever sell this building, offer it first to the people who live in it. They made it a home. I only made it a building. Sal."
EDDIE: He wrote that three years ago. And tomorrow, we're doing it. That's... that's something.
PRIYA: Diego, is the drawing ready? We need it in the envelope.
DIEGO: Almost. I'm doing the windows. Every window has a person. Look. That's Eddie with his radio. That's Mrs. Esposito with Biscuit.
EDDIE: Is that me? I look very handsome.
DIEGO: I drew you younger. Like the lake. Nineteen seventy.
PRIYA: And who's on the roof?
DIEGO: Everybody else. Mom, Tommy, Gina, Luca, Sam, you, Marcus, Tasha, Jesse, Tess. And the land trust lady. Nadia. She's holding the land.
GINA: She's holding the land? Like, in her hands?
DIEGO: The land is a green square. She's holding it. Under the building. So it doesn't float away.
PRIYA: Where's Biscuit? In the drawing?
DIEGO: In every window. She moves around. Like in real life.
EDDIE: That cat has been in every apartment in this building. She's the real owner.
EDDIE: That's the best explanation of a land trust I've ever heard. Put it in the envelope, Gina. Before a lawyer sees it.
MARISOL: Wait. What are you all doing? It's eleven thirty. Diego, bed.
DIEGO: Mom! I'm finishing the drawing! It's for the bid! It's important!
MARISOL: It's beautiful, mijo. Really. Okay. Five more minutes. Then bed.
GINA: Marisol, the cover letter. You have to sign it. As president. Read it first.
MARISOL: Okay. "Dear Mr. Whitlock. Enclosed is our best and final offer. It is not the highest number you will receive. But it is the most certain. And it comes with the people who made your building worth buying."

MARISOL: My hand is shaking. Look at it.
PRIYA: Gina's trick. Put it under the table. Then sign.
MARISOL: You can't sign under a table.
GINA: Then sign it shaking. That's honest too.
MARISOL: Okay. Signed. Marisol Reyes. President. February twenty-fifth. Eleven thirty-four p.m.
DIEGO: Can I sign too? Under you? Small?
MARISOL: ...Yes. Small. "Diego Reyes. Treasurer. In the future."

GINA: Is she answering?
TOMMY: She's saying, "Tommy, don't touch." Like always.
GINA: Don't touch what?
TOMMY: Anything. The envelope. The number. The plan. She's saying, "You did your part. Now let it go."
GINA: I'm taking it to Harborline's office at nine. Marisol and Nadia are coming with me. We'll hand it to the receptionist. In person.
TOMMY: In person. Good. Good news comes by text. Important news comes in person.
GINA: Dad, can I ask you something? If we lose. What will you do?
TOMMY: If we lose? I'll pour beer until May thirty-first. Then I'll pour beer somewhere else. Maybe a food truck. "The Hook on Wheels."
GINA: You'd hate a food truck.
TOMMY: I'd hate it. But I'd hate it with you. That's something.
GINA: Okay. I'll take that.
GINA: Dad. Whatever happens. You fought. I've never seen you fight like this.

TOMMY: In a minute. One more thing. Angela, if you're listening. Tomorrow, noon. Give us a little luck. Just a little. We did the rest.
GINA: She's listening. She always listened. Even when you didn't think so.
TOMMY: Okay. Lights off. It's now or never.

TOMMY: That's not her rule.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **표결 진행**
  - *Do we have a quorum?* — "정족수가 찼습니까?"
  - *The motion is to submit…* — "안건은 ~을 제출하는 것입니다."
  - *I so move.* — "그렇게 동의합니다." 동의를 내는 격식 표현.
  - *Is there any discussion?* — "토론하실 분 계십니까?"
  - *Let the record show: the motion carries.* — "회의록에 기록해 주십시오. 안건은 가결되었습니다."
  - *It's unanimous.* — "만장일치입니다."
- **확신과 불안**
  - *I'm voting yes. But I'm voting yes with my eyes open.* — 찬성하지만 위험을 알고 찬성한다.
  - *It's now or never.* — "지금 아니면 영영 못 해."
  - *If we lose, it's nobody's fault.* — 미리 탓을 덜어 주는 공동체의 말.
- **경쟁 입찰**
  - *We don't try to beat their price. We beat their offer.* — 가격이 아니라 조건 전체로 이긴다.
  - *It is not the highest number. But it is the most certain.* — 가장 높지는 않지만 가장 확실한 제안.
  - *enclosed* 동봉된 / *financing letters* 자금 확약서 / *cover letter* 첨부 편지
- **the Buckner game** — 1986년 월드시리즈 6차전. 메츠가 극적으로 이긴 경기. 토미의 인생 경기(7화).
- **CLOSED FOR DEMOCRACY** — "민주주의를 위해 휴업." 바 문에 붙인 농담.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- 표결 장면은 의식처럼, 천천히. *All in favor?* 다음에 각 사람의 *Aye*가 하나씩 떨어진다. 에디의 *Aye*가 가장 느리고 무겁다.
- 부인의 회의록 낭독은 시각까지 정확하게: *nine oh two p.m.* / *nine oh nine p.m.*
- 숫자: *three point three five* / *three point three oh five* — *oh*는 0. 
- 살의 편지는 지나가 천천히, 거의 속삭이듯 읽는다.
- 마지막 장면은 새벽 1시. 아주 작은 소리. 토미의 *Give us a little luck. | Just a little.*
- *It's now or never.* — *now*에 강세. 지나의 *Never is not an option.*은 미소를 머금고.
