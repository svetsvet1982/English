# THE CORNER HOUSE — 2부 30화

## The F Train Is Down (F선이 멈췄다)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 2부 12화(통산 30화) · 시기: 2026년 7월 말, 월요일 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: Van Brunt Street의 B61 버스 정류장 → 레드훅 페리 선착장과 페리 위 → 저녁, 바 The Hook
> - 등장인물: GINA, MARCUS, KEISHA, TOMMY, TASHA
> - 사건: 지나가 병가 후 복귀하는 첫날, 신호 고장으로 F선이 양방향 운행 중단된다. 버스, 시티바이크, 우버, 페리를 놓고 대안을 고르다 마커스·키샤와 함께 꽉 찬 페리를 탄다.
> - 학습 포인트: 교통 어휘, 지연과 대안(instead, otherwise, alternate routes), 비상 대응, 안내 방송 말투
> - 미국 현안: 지하철 신호 노후화와 지연, 맨해튼 혼잡통행료(2025년 1월 시행, 승용차 낮 9달러), 할증 요금, 레드훅의 교통 사각지대
> - 연결: 지나는 5시 정시 퇴근 성공. CEO 브래드는 택시에서 혼잡통행료를 내고도 지각. 토미의 1984년 F선 등굣길 이야기.

## 대본

[Scene: Monday, 7:40 a.m. The B61 bus stop on Van Brunt Street. Hot already.]

GINA: Marcus! Good morning. You're up early today, even for you.
MARCUS: I have to pick up some bike parts in Manhattan, from a shop in Chinatown, and I need to get there before ten.
GINA: Me too. Well, not Chinatown, Midtown. It's my first day back after the week off.
MARCUS: So how do you feel? Be honest.
GINA: Better, much better. I slept, I saw a doctor, and I talked to a counselor twice.
MARCUS: That's great, Gina. Really, I mean it. You look like you finally got some rest.
GINA: And today, I'm going to leave at five, not seven. That's my plan, and I'm sticking to it.
MARCUS: Good plan. But where's the bus? It's late again.
GINA: The app says seven minutes. But it said seven minutes ten minutes ago, too.
MARCUS: The B Sixty-One, the most famous bus in Red Hook. It's famous for never coming.
GINA: Oh no. Look at this. I just got a notification from the MTA.
MARCUS: What does it say? Is it about our bus?
GINA: "F trains are suspended in both directions between Jay Street and Church Avenue. Due to a signal problem."
MARCUS: Suspended? In both directions? But that's our train!
GINA: And then it says, "Expect long delays. Consider alternate routes."
MARCUS: Consider alternate routes. What alternate routes? We're in Red Hook, and there's no subway here anyway.
GINA: Okay, let's think about this. What can we do instead?
MARCUS: Option one. We take the bus to the A train at Jay Street. But everyone will do that, so it'll be packed.
GINA: Option two. Citi Bike. There's a station right here on Van Brunt.
MARCUS: In this heat? All the way to Midtown? You'll arrive looking like you swam there.
GINA: Option three. We get an Uber.
MARCUS: Check the price first, before you get excited.
GINA: Sixty-eight dollars, because of surge pricing. Plus the congestion toll on top of that.
MARCUS: Sixty-eight! For one ride! Otherwise, it's just one subway fare.
GINA: Option four. We take the ferry from the Red Hook pier to Wall Street.
MARCUS: The ferry! That's only twenty-five minutes, and then we take the subway from Wall Street. The other lines are okay, right?
GINA: Let me check. The 4, the 5... yes, good service. Okay, so the ferry, and then the 4 train.
MARCUS: When's the next ferry? They don't run that often.
GINA: The next one is at seven fifty-eight, which is in nine minutes. Can we walk there in nine minutes?
MARCUS: If we walk fast. Like real New Yorkers, with our elbows out.
GINA: Then let's go right now. We'll run if we have to, okay?
MARCUS: You just came back from sick leave, so don't run. Just walk fast.
GINA: Okay. Walk fast. Very fast.

[Scene: 7:55 a.m. The ferry dock. A long line along the water.]

MARCUS: Whoa. Look at this line. It goes all the way down the pier, so everybody had the same idea.
GINA: Of course they did. The F is down, so the whole neighborhood is here.
KEISHA: Gina! Marcus! You're stuck too?
GINA: Keisha! Yes, it's the F. Are you going to the hospital?
KEISHA: Yes, my shift starts at nine in Lower Manhattan. If I'm late, the night nurse can't go home, and she's been working all night.
MARCUS: Will we all fit on one ferry, though? That's a lot of people.
KEISHA: The ferry holds about one fifty, and I count... maybe a hundred and eighty people.
GINA: So thirty people stay behind and wait thirty minutes for the next one.
KEISHA: Then let's not be the thirty. Stay close to me, and don't let anyone cut in front.
MARCUS: Here it comes. Okay, move, move. Keep walking.
GINA: We're on! We made it!
KEISHA: Barely. They closed the gate right behind us, so we were the last ones on.
MARCUS: Look at the people still on the dock. They look so sad, like they just missed a birthday party.
GINA: That was almost me. Last month, I would have had a panic attack right now, just from watching this.
KEISHA: And today? How are you feeling?
GINA: Today, I'm breathing. In and out. I'll be late, and that's okay.
KEISHA: Good for you, seriously. Being late is not the end of the world.
MARCUS: Tell that to my parts guy. He closes at ten, and he doesn't wait for anybody.
KEISHA: Why don't you just drive in with a van next time?
MARCUS: My friend's van? All the way to Chinatown? With the congestion pricing, it's expensive.
KEISHA: How much is it now? I always forget.
MARCUS: For a car, it's nine dollars a day during the day, and for a small truck, it's more. My friend pays it every day, and he hates it.
GINA: But the traffic is better, at least according to the reports. There are fewer cars, and the buses are faster.
MARCUS: The buses are faster in Manhattan, maybe, but not in Red Hook. Our bus is still a ghost.
KEISHA: Honestly, I like it. The streets near the hospital are quieter, so the ambulances get through faster.
MARCUS: Okay, that's a good point. Ambulances are important.
GINA: And the money goes to the subway, so they can fix things like signals.
MARCUS: Like the signal that broke this morning?
GINA: Okay. Not that fast. Fixing a hundred-year-old system takes time.
KEISHA: My dad told me the F train signals are from the nineteen thirties.
MARCUS: That's older than Eddie!
GINA: Don't tell Eddie that, because he'll be offended.
KEISHA: Look, there's the Statue of Liberty. Good morning, lady. You look great for your age.
MARCUS: Actually, this is a commute I could get used to. If the train breaks every day, I'll take the ferry every day.
GINA: The ferry is the best-kept secret in New York. Most people don't even know it exists.
KEISHA: Well, it's not a secret today. Today, everybody knows.
MARCUS: Okay, we're arriving at Wall Street. So then what?
GINA: Then I take the 4 to Grand Central, and I walk from there.
KEISHA: I walk from here, because it's only ten minutes. Good luck, everybody.
MARCUS: I'll take the J to Canal Street. If the J works.
GINA: If the J works. That's the New York prayer.
KEISHA: Text me if you get stuck anywhere. Bye!
MARCUS: Bye, Keisha! Gina, good luck on your first day back.
GINA: Thanks, Marcus. Breathe in, breathe out. That's my plan for today.
MARCUS: And leave at five. You promised, and I'm going to ask Tommy.

[Scene: 6:10 p.m. The Hook. Gina walks in with her bag.]

TOMMY: There she is! So how was the first day back?
GINA: It started with the F train, which was suspended because of a signal problem.
TASHA: I saw it on the news. Thousands of people were stuck, and some people even walked on the tracks.
TOMMY: On the tracks? That's crazy, and it's dangerous.
GINA: We took the ferry, me and Marcus and Keisha. Half of Red Hook was on that ferry.
TOMMY: The ferry! That's smart. So how late were you?
GINA: Fifty minutes. I walked into my first meeting at ten fifty, still a little out of breath.
TASHA: And your boss? Did Craig give you some of his "candid feedback"?
GINA: He said, "Welcome back. The F is down, right? Don't worry about it."
TASHA: That's it? That's all he said?
GINA: That's all. Half the office was late, and even Brad was late. He was stuck in a cab on the FDR.
TOMMY: Ha! The CEO, stuck in traffic in a cab, and paying the congestion toll for it.
GINA: He arrived at eleven fifteen, which was later than me.
TASHA: I love that. I love that so much, I want it on a T-shirt.
TOMMY: And you? How did you feel today, really?
GINA: Honestly? It was okay, and I was calm the whole time. On the ferry, I looked at the water, and I just breathed.
TOMMY: That's my girl.
GINA: And I left at five, exactly five. I just walked out, and nobody said anything.
TASHA: Five! Gina Russo left the office at five o'clock!
TOMMY: Write it on the board, right next to "phone off at Rockaway."
GINA: And the trip home was fine. The F was working again, mostly, although there were still twenty-minute delays.
TOMMY: In the eighties, the subway was much worse. There was graffiti everywhere, and the trains broke down every day.
TASHA: Uh-oh. Here comes the history lesson.
TOMMY: It's true! I took the F to high school in nineteen eighty-four, and sometimes it took two hours.
GINA: Two hours? For what, six stops?
TOMMY: For six stops. In the summer, there was no air conditioning, and in the winter, there was no heat.
TASHA: And you still went to school every day?
TOMMY: If I didn't go, my mother would have killed me, so yes, I went.
GINA: So today was nothing, compared to nineteen eighty-four.
TOMMY: Today was a vacation. You got a ferry ride with the Statue of Liberty.
TASHA: What about Marcus? Did he get his parts in time?
GINA: He texted me. The J was delayed, so he got there at ten oh five, and the shop was already closed.
TOMMY: Closed! After all that!
GINA: But the owner saw him through the window, and he opened the door for him, because Marcus brought him a coffee.
TASHA: Ha! That's how you do it. Coffee opens doors.
TOMMY: My coffee opens doors too, you know.
TASHA: Your coffee closes doors, Tommy. It closes whole restaurants.
GINA: Dad, I want to say something. Thank you for the ice bucket, and for everything last week.
TOMMY: You don't have to thank me, sweetheart. That's what fathers are for, and you'd do the same for Luca.
GINA: I know. But I'm saying it anyway, because today was a good day, even with the F train.
TOMMY: Especially with the F train. You handled it. You stayed calm, like a real New Yorker.
GINA: A real New Yorker. Late, but calm, and home by six.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **교통 안내 표현** — *F trains are suspended in both directions*(양방향 운행 중단), *due to a signal problem*(신호 문제로), *Expect long delays.*(긴 지연 예상), *Consider alternate routes.*(다른 경로를 고려하세요), *Good service*(정상 운행).
- **대안 말하기** — *What can we do instead? / Option one… Option two… / Otherwise, it's just one subway fare.*(그렇지 않으면 = 지하철로 가면)
- **교통 어휘** — *the B61*(버스 노선), *Citi Bike*(공공 자전거), *surge pricing*(할증 요금), *the congestion toll*(혼잡통행료), *the ferry dock*, *a commute*(통근), *the tracks*(선로).
- **We made it! / Barely.** — "탔다!" / "아슬아슬하게."
- **stay behind** — 뒤에 남다.
- **the best-kept secret** — 사람들이 잘 모르는 숨은 보물.
- **That's my girl.** — "역시 우리 딸."
- **You handled it.** — "잘 대처했어."
- **I would have had a panic attack.** — "(예전 같았으면) 공황이 왔을 거야." 과거 사실과 반대되는 가정.
- **Not that fast.** — "그렇게 빨리는 안 돼."
- **the New York prayer** — "뉴욕식 기도." "If the J works(J선이 다니면)."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **안내 방송 흉내**: *"F trains are suspended in both directions…"* — 감정 없이 일정한 높이로 또박또박. 그 뒤 마커스의 반응은 크게.
- 노선 이름은 글자·숫자 그대로: *the F* [디 에f], *the 4 train* [더 포r 트레인], *the J* [더 제이], *the B Sixty-One*.
- **alternate** [**올**터r넛](형용사) — 동사 alternate [올터r네잇]과 발음이 다르다.
- **suspended** [서스**펜**디드], **congestion** [컨**제**스천], **notification** [노우리피**케**이션].
- *Walk fast. Very fast.* — 걸음처럼 짧고 빠르게.
- 지나의 *Breathe in, breathe out.*은 29화와 이어진다. 차분하게.
