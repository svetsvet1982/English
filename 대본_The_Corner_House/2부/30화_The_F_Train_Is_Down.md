# THE CORNER HOUSE — 2부 30화

## The F Train Is Down (F선이 멈췄다)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 2부 12화(통산 30화) · 시기: 2026년 7월 말, 월요일 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: Van Brunt Street의 B61 버스 정류장 → 레드훅 페리 선착장과 페리 위 → 저녁, 바 The Hook
> - 등장인물: GINA, MARCUS, KEISHA, TOMMY, TASHA
> - 사건: 지나가 병가 후 복귀하는 첫날, 신호 고장으로 F선이 양방향 운행 중단된다. 버스, 시티바이크, 우버, 페리를 놓고 대안을 고르다 마커스·키샤와 함께 꽉 찬 페리를 탄다.
> - 학습 포인트: 교통 어휘, 지연과 대안(instead, otherwise, alternate routes), 비상 대응, 안내 방송 말투
> - 미국 현안: 지하철 신호 노후화와 지연, 맨해튼 혼잡통행료(2025년 1월 시행, 승용차 낮 9달러), 할증 요금, 레드훅의 교통 사각지대
> - 연결: 지나는 5시 정시 퇴근 성공. CEO 브래드는 택시에서 혼잡통행료를 내고도 지각. 토미의 1984년 F선 등굣길 이야기.

## 대본

[Scene: Monday, 7:40 a.m. The B61 bus stop on Van Brunt Street. Hot already.]

GINA: Marcus! Good morning. You're up early.
MARCUS: I have to pick up parts in Manhattan. A shop in Chinatown. Before ten.
GINA: Me too. Well, not Chinatown. Midtown. My first day back after the week off.
MARCUS: How do you feel?
GINA: Better. Much better. I slept. I saw a doctor. I talked to a counselor. Twice.
MARCUS: That's great, Gina. Really.
GINA: And today, I'm going to leave at five. Not seven. That's my plan.
MARCUS: Good plan. Where's the bus? It's late.
GINA: The app says seven minutes. It said seven minutes ten minutes ago.
MARCUS: The B Sixty-One. The most famous bus in Red Hook. Famous for never coming.
GINA: Oh no. Look at this. A notification. From the MTA.
MARCUS: What does it say?
GINA: "F trains are suspended in both directions between Jay Street and Church Avenue. Due to a signal problem."
MARCUS: Suspended? Both directions? That's our train!
GINA: "Expect long delays. Consider alternate routes."
MARCUS: Consider alternate routes. What alternate routes? We're in Red Hook. There's no subway here anyway.
GINA: Okay, think. What can we do instead?
MARCUS: Option one. Take the bus to the A train. At Jay Street. But everyone will do that. It'll be packed.
GINA: Option two. Citi Bike. There's a station on Van Brunt.
MARCUS: In this heat? To Midtown? You'll arrive like you swam there.
GINA: Option three. An Uber.
MARCUS: Check the price.
GINA: Sixty-eight dollars. Surge pricing. Plus the congestion toll.
MARCUS: Sixty-eight! For one ride! Otherwise, it's just one subway fare.
GINA: Option four. The ferry. From the Red Hook pier. To Wall Street.
MARCUS: The ferry! Twenty-five minutes. Then the subway from Wall Street. The other lines are okay, right?
GINA: Let me check. The 4, the 5... yes. Good service. Okay. The ferry, then the 4 train.
MARCUS: When's the next ferry?
GINA: Seven fifty-eight. In nine minutes. Can we walk there in nine minutes?
MARCUS: If we walk fast. Like New Yorkers.
GINA: Then let's go. Run if we have to.
MARCUS: You just came back from sick leave. Don't run. Walk fast.
GINA: Okay. Walk fast. Very fast.

[Scene: 7:55 a.m. The ferry dock. A long line along the water.]

MARCUS: Whoa. Look at this line. Everybody had the same idea.
GINA: Of course. The F is down. The whole neighborhood is here.
KEISHA: Gina! Marcus! You too?
GINA: Keisha! Yes. The F. Are you going to the hospital?
KEISHA: Yes. My shift starts at nine. In Lower Manhattan. If I'm late, the night nurse can't leave.
MARCUS: Will we all fit? On one ferry?
KEISHA: The ferry holds about one fifty. I count... maybe a hundred and eighty people.
GINA: So thirty people stay behind. And wait for the next one. Thirty minutes.
KEISHA: Then let's not be the thirty. Stay close.
MARCUS: Here it comes. Okay. Move, move.
GINA: We're on! We made it!
KEISHA: Barely. They closed the gate right after us.
MARCUS: Look at the people on the dock. They look so sad.
GINA: That was almost me. Last month, I would have had a panic attack right now.
KEISHA: And today?
GINA: Today, I'm breathing. In and out. I'll be late. And that's okay.
KEISHA: Good for you. Seriously. Late is not the end of the world.
MARCUS: Tell that to my parts guy. He closes at ten.
KEISHA: Why don't you just drive in? With a van?
MARCUS: My friend's van? To Chinatown? With the congestion pricing? It's expensive.
KEISHA: How much is it now?
MARCUS: For a car, nine dollars a day. During the day. For a small truck, more. My friend pays it every day. He hates it.
GINA: But the traffic is better. That's what the reports say. Fewer cars. Faster buses.
MARCUS: Faster buses in Manhattan. Not in Red Hook. Our bus is still a ghost.
KEISHA: I like it, honestly. The streets near the hospital are quieter. The ambulances get through faster.
MARCUS: That's a good point. Ambulances are important.
GINA: And the money goes to the subway. To fix things. Like signals.
MARCUS: Like the signal that broke this morning?
GINA: Okay. Not that fast. Fixing a hundred-year-old system takes time.
KEISHA: The F train signals are from the nineteen thirties. My dad told me.
MARCUS: Older than Eddie!
GINA: Don't tell Eddie that. He'll be offended.
KEISHA: Look. The Statue of Liberty. Good morning, lady.
MARCUS: Not a bad commute, actually. If the train breaks every day, I'll take the ferry every day.
GINA: The ferry is the best-kept secret in New York.
KEISHA: Not a secret today. Today, everybody knows.
MARCUS: Okay. We're arriving. Wall Street. Then what?
GINA: Then I take the 4 to Grand Central. Then I walk.
KEISHA: I walk from here. Ten minutes. Good luck, everybody.
MARCUS: I'll take the J to Canal Street. If the J works.
GINA: If the J works. That's the New York prayer.
KEISHA: Text me if you're stuck. Bye!
MARCUS: Bye, Keisha! Gina, good luck on your first day back.
GINA: Thanks, Marcus. Breathe in, breathe out.
MARCUS: And leave at five. You promised.

[Scene: 6:10 p.m. The Hook. Gina walks in with her bag.]

TOMMY: There she is! How was the first day back?
GINA: It started with the F train. Suspended. A signal problem.
TASHA: I saw it on the news. Thousands of people stuck. Some people walked on the tracks.
TOMMY: On the tracks? That's crazy. That's dangerous.
GINA: We took the ferry. Me, Marcus, Keisha. Half of Red Hook was on that ferry.
TOMMY: The ferry! Smart. How late were you?
GINA: Fifty minutes. I walked into my first meeting at ten fifty.
TASHA: And your boss? Did Craig give you "candid feedback"?
GINA: He said, "Welcome back. The F is down, right? Don't worry about it."
TASHA: That's it? That's all?
GINA: That's all. Half the office was late. Brad was late too. He was stuck in a cab on the FDR.
TOMMY: Ha! The CEO in a cab. Paying the congestion toll. Stuck in traffic.
GINA: He arrived at eleven fifteen. Later than me.
TASHA: I love that. I love that so much.
TOMMY: And you? How did you feel? Today?
GINA: Honestly? Okay. I was calm. On the ferry, I looked at the water. I breathed.
TOMMY: That's my girl.
GINA: And I left at five. Exactly five. I walked out. Nobody said anything.
TASHA: Five! Gina Russo left the office at five!
TOMMY: Write it on the board! Next to "phone off at Rockaway."
GINA: And the trip home? The F was working again. Mostly. Twenty-minute delays.
TOMMY: In the eighties, the subway was worse. Much worse. Graffiti everywhere. Trains broke down every day.
TASHA: Here comes the history lesson.
TOMMY: It's true! I took the F to high school. In nineteen eighty-four. Sometimes it took two hours.
GINA: Two hours? For what? Six stops?
TOMMY: For six stops. In the summer, no air conditioning. In the winter, no heat.
TASHA: And you still went to school?
TOMMY: My mother would have killed me. So, yes.
GINA: So today was nothing. Compared to nineteen eighty-four.
TOMMY: Today was a vacation. A ferry ride with the Statue of Liberty.
TASHA: What about Marcus? Did he get his parts?
GINA: He texted me. The J was delayed. He got there at ten oh five. The shop was closed.
TOMMY: Closed! After all that!
GINA: But the owner saw him through the window. He opened the door for him. Because Marcus brought him a coffee.
TASHA: Ha! That's how you do it. Coffee opens doors.
TOMMY: My coffee opens doors too.
TASHA: Your coffee closes doors, Tommy.
GINA: Dad, I want to say something. Thank you. For the ice bucket. And for last week.
TOMMY: You don't have to thank me.
GINA: I know. But I'm saying it anyway. Today was a good day. Even with the F train.
TOMMY: Especially with the F train. You handled it. Calm. Like a real New Yorker.
GINA: A real New Yorker. Late, but calm. And home by six.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **교통 안내 표현** — *F trains are suspended in both directions*(양방향 운행 중단), *due to a signal problem*(신호 문제로), *Expect long delays.*(긴 지연 예상), *Consider alternate routes.*(다른 경로를 고려하세요), *Good service*(정상 운행).
- **대안 말하기** — *What can we do instead? / Option one… Option two… / Otherwise, it's just one subway fare.*(그렇지 않으면 = 지하철로 가면)
- **교통 어휘** — *the B61*(버스 노선), *Citi Bike*(공공 자전거), *surge pricing*(할증 요금), *the congestion toll*(혼잡통행료), *the ferry dock*, *a commute*(통근), *the tracks*(선로).
- **We made it! / Barely.** — "탔다!" / "아슬아슬하게."
- **stay behind** — 뒤에 남다.
- **the best-kept secret** — 사람들이 잘 모르는 숨은 보물.
- **That's my girl.** — "역시 우리 딸."
- **You handled it.** — "잘 대처했어."
- **I would have had a panic attack.** — "(예전 같았으면) 공황이 왔을 거야." 과거 사실과 반대되는 가정.
- **Not that fast.** — "그렇게 빨리는 안 돼."
- **the New York prayer** — "뉴욕식 기도." "If the J works(J선이 다니면)."

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **안내 방송 흉내**: *"F trains are suspended in both directions…"* — 감정 없이 일정한 높이로 또박또박. 그 뒤 마커스의 반응은 크게.
- 노선 이름은 글자·숫자 그대로: *the F* [디 에f], *the 4 train* [더 포r 트레인], *the J* [더 제이], *the B Sixty-One*.
- **alternate** [**올**터r넛](형용사) — 동사 alternate [올터r네잇]과 발음이 다르다.
- **suspended** [서스**펜**디드], **congestion** [컨**제**스천], **notification** [노우리피**케**이션].
- *Walk fast. Very fast.* — 걸음처럼 짧고 빠르게.
- 지나의 *Breathe in, breathe out.*은 29화와 이어진다. 차분하게.
