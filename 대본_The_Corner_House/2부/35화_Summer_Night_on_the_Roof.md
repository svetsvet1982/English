# THE CORNER HOUSE — 2부 35화

## Summer Night on the Roof (옥상의 여름밤)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 2부 17화(통산 35화) · 시기: 2026년 8월 말, 토요일 밤 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 건물 옥상 (긴 식탁, 촛불, 맨해튼 야경)
> - 등장인물: MRS. ESPOSITO, DIEGO, MARISOL, TOMMY, EDDIE, PRIYA, JESSE, SAM
> - 사건: 에스포지토 부인이 13화부터 숨겨 온 계획을 실행한다. 어머니의 접시 열두 개와 한 번도 쓰지 않은 식탁보로 건물 사람들을 옥상 만찬에 초대한다. 토미와 30년 앙금을 풀고, 젊은 이웃들은 "Have you ever…?" 게임을 하다 집을 잃을까 두려운 마음을 꺼낸다.
> - 학습 포인트: 회상, 화해 표현(No hard feelings / I'm apologizing for the tone), 현재완료 경험(Have you ever…? / I've never… / I've lived here for…)
> - 미국 현안: 불안정한 주거 속 '오래 머물 곳'에 대한 갈망, 이웃 공동체
> - 연결: 디에고의 소원 "이사 가지 않게 해 주세요" → 36화 매각 확정. 에디는 1976년 200주년 불꽃놀이를 이 옥상에서 봤다. 에스포지토 부인은 이 건물에 31년째.

## 대본

[Scene: Saturday, 7:30 p.m. The roof. A long table with a white tablecloth. Mrs. Esposito sets out blue plates.]

MRS. ESPOSITO: Careful with those plates, Diego. They're from Italy. Nineteen fifty-five.
DIEGO: I'm being so careful. I'm walking like a robot.
MARISOL: Dolores, this is beautiful. The lights, the flowers. The tablecloth.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The tablecloth was my mother's too. I've never used it. Not once. Until tonight.
MARISOL: Never? Why not?
MRS. ESPOSITO: I was always waiting for a special occasion. And then I waited thirty years.
DIEGO: Is tonight a special occasion?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Tonight, I decided it is. That's how special occasions work. You decide.
MARISOL: How many people are coming?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Twelve. Twelve plates. Exactly twelve. I counted three times.
DIEGO: Who's coming?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Everybody in the building. Eddie, Tommy, Gina and Luca. Priya and Sam. You two. Jesse. And Tasha and Marcus. They're almost the building.
MARISOL: That's... eleven. Plus you, twelve.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Plus me. Twelve. I've done the math. I'm the secretary.
DIEGO: Does Tommy know?
MRS. ESPOSITO: Tommy thinks he's coming to fix my air conditioner. On the roof.
MARISOL: Ha! He's going to be so surprised.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Good. Tommy surprised me with eight-dollar beer. Now I surprise him.
DIEGO: What are we eating?
MRS. ESPOSITO: My lasagna. Two trays. And Tasha is bringing empanadas. And Priya is bringing samosas.
DIEGO: Lasagna and empanadas and samosas!
MARISOL: That's the most New York dinner I've ever heard of.
MRS. ESPOSITO: That's the point. Okay. Candles on the table. Diego, the matches are in my bag.
DIEGO: Can I light them?
MARISOL: With me. Carefully.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Here they come. I hear Eddie on the stairs. He sings when he climbs.
MARISOL: What does he sing?
MRS. ESPOSITO: The same song. Every time. "Danny Boy." Very slowly. One step, one word.
DIEGO: Shh! They're coming! Hide!
MARISOL: We can't hide, Diego. We're on a roof.

[The roof door opens. Tommy carries a toolbox. Eddie is behind him.]

TOMMY: Dolores? Where's the air conditioner? You said it was broken... Whoa.
EDDIE: Surprise, Tommy!
TOMMY: What is this? A table? Candles? Lasagna?
MRS. ESPOSITO: It's a dinner. For the building. My twelve plates. Finally used.
TOMMY: Dolores Esposito made a dinner party. For us. I've never seen this before. In twenty years.
MRS. ESPOSITO: You've never been invited before. Sit. You're next to me.
TOMMY: Next to you? Should I be worried?
MRS. ESPOSITO: A little. Sit.
PRIYA: Dolores, this is incredible. Thank you for inviting us. I brought samosas. My mom's recipe.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Thank you, dear. Put them next to the empanadas. They'll be friends.
EDDIE: I haven't been on this roof since the Bicentennial. Nineteen seventy-six. Peggy and I watched the fireworks from here.
PRIYA: From here? Not the pier?
EDDIE: The pier was for the ships. The roof was for the fireworks. You could see everything. Manhattan. The Statue. The whole harbor.
TOMMY: Look at it now. Look at Manhattan. All lit up.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Okay. Before we eat. I want to say something. Tommy, don't make a face.
TOMMY: I didn't make a face.
MRS. ESPOSITO: You were going to. Listen. I've lived in this building for thirty-one years. I've complained a lot.
EDDIE: A lot.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Eddie. I've complained about the noise. The sign. The trash. The beer prices.
TOMMY: The sign. Two feet is two feet.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Two feet is two feet. That's still true. But this summer, I learned something.
PRIYA: What did you learn?
MRS. ESPOSITO: When the note came, nobody complained. Everybody helped. Marisol. Kyle. Jesse. Tommy. Even the cat.
EDDIE: Especially the cat.
MRS. ESPOSITO: So tonight, I want to say thank you. And I'm sorry. For all the complaining.
TOMMY: Dolores... You don't have to apologize.
MRS. ESPOSITO: I'm not apologizing for the rules. The rules were right. I'm apologizing for the tone.
TOMMY: Ha. Okay. Then I'm sorry too. For the eight-dollar beer. And for the sign.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Are you going to move the sign?
TOMMY: No. But I'm sorry about it.
MRS. ESPOSITO: That's very Brooklyn. No hard feelings.
TOMMY: No hard feelings. Truly.
EDDIE: Now kiss and make up.
MRS. ESPOSITO: Eddie! Absolutely not.
PRIYA: Can I add something? Sam and I have only been here two years. But this summer, it finally felt like home.
TOMMY: Two years is enough. You're family now. You suffer with us.
PRIYA: We'll suffer with you. Happily.
EDDIE: To the building. Twelve plates. One building.
TOMMY: To the building. And to Dolores. The best secretary on Van Brunt Street.
MRS. ESPOSITO: The only secretary on Van Brunt Street.
PRIYA: To Dolores!
EDDIE: Now, the lasagna. Before it gets cold. My stomach has been waiting since the stairs.
TOMMY: Dolores, this lasagna... This is incredible. Who taught you?
MRS. ESPOSITO: My mother. With these plates. In nineteen sixty-two. The year my brother broke one.
EDDIE: The famous chip!

[Scene: 10:00 p.m. The older neighbors have gone down. The young ones sit at the end of the table.]

JESSE: Okay, who wants to play a game? Tess taught me this one. "Have you ever."
SAM: How does it work?
JESSE: Someone asks, "Have you ever...?" And everyone answers. Honestly.
DIEGO: Can I play?
MARISOL: Only the kid version.
JESSE: Okay. Kid version. I'll start. Have you ever been to the top of the Empire State Building?
DIEGO: Yes! In third grade. With my class. It was so windy.
PRIYA: Never. I've lived in New York for twelve years. I've never been up there.
SAM: Me neither. Locals never go. Only tourists.
MARISOL: I went once. In two thousand nine. On a first date. The guy was afraid of heights.
JESSE: On a first date? Was it Diego's dad?
MARISOL: No. Thank God. That guy cried in the elevator.
DIEGO: Ha! He cried!
SAM: My turn. Have you ever eaten a whole pizza by yourself?
JESSE: Yes. Last year. A very bad week.
DIEGO: Not yet. But I want to.
MARISOL: Not in this house, you won't.
PRIYA: Have you ever lived somewhere for more than ten years?
MARISOL: Yes. This building. Ten years. Since Diego was born.
SAM: Never. I moved every two years. College, then jobs. This is the longest I've stayed anywhere. As an adult.
JESSE: Never. Not since I left home. College, then four apartments in five years. And a couch.
DIEGO: My whole life! I've lived here my whole life!
PRIYA: That's the dream, right? To stay somewhere. For a long time.
SAM: That's why we want to stay. After the wedding. If they let us.
MARISOL: If they let us. That's the question for all of us now.
JESSE: Have you ever... been scared of losing your home?
MARISOL: Jesse, that's not the kid version.
DIEGO: I'm scared. A little. When Mom reads letters at night. She makes a face.
MARISOL: Oh, baby. I didn't know you saw that.
DIEGO: I see everything. Like Tommy. I'm a bartender.
PRIYA: Ha! He's a bartender.
MARISOL: Listen, Diego. Whatever happens, we're together. And the building is together now. Look at this table.
DIEGO: Twelve plates.
MARISOL: Twelve plates. One building.
SAM: Have you ever seen a shooting star? Look! Up there! Did you see it?
DIEGO: I saw it! I saw it! Over Manhattan!
JESSE: Make a wish. Everybody. Quick.
PRIYA: Done.
SAM: Done.
MARISOL: Done. Diego?
DIEGO: I can't say it. Or it won't come true.
JESSE: Eddie says on the roof, you can say it. Roof rules.
DIEGO: Okay. I wish we never have to move.
MARISOL: Me too, baby. Me too.
JESSE: Then that's the wish. For all twelve plates.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **현재완료로 경험 말하기** — *Have you ever been to…? / I've never been up there. / I've never used it. / I've lived in this building for thirty-one years. / This is the longest I've stayed anywhere.* 과거의 특정 시점 없이 '지금까지'의 경험을 말할 때.
- **과거형과의 차이** — 시점이 분명하면 과거형. *I went once. In two thousand nine.*
- **Me neither.** — "나도 (안 해 봤어)." 부정문에 동의할 때. 긍정이면 Me too.
- **화해 표현** — *I'm sorry. For all the complaining. / I'm not apologizing for the rules. I'm apologizing for the tone. / No hard feelings.*(앙금 없어요) / *kiss and make up*(화해하다, 원래는 '키스하고 화해하다'라는 관용구).
- **a special occasion** — 특별한 날. *That's how special occasions work. You decide.*
- **don't make a face** — 인상 쓰지 마.
- **You suffer with us.** — "이제 우리랑 같이 고생하는 거야." 가족으로 받아들이는 농담.
- **Not in this house, you won't.** — "이 집에선 안 될걸." 부모의 단호한 말투.
- **a shooting star / make a wish** — 별똥별 / 소원을 빌다.
- **Roof rules.** — "옥상 규칙이야." 즉석에서 만든 규칙.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **Have you ever…?** [해뷰 에버r] — have you가 [해뷰]로 붙는다. 끝을 올린다.
- **I've never** [아이브 네버r] — 've를 분명히. *I never…*로 들리지 않도록.
- 부인의 연설은 한 문장씩 끊어서, 진심을 담아 천천히. *I'm apologizing for the tone.*에서 미소.
- **No hard feelings. / No hard feelings. Truly.** — 두 사람이 같은 말을 주고받는다. 두 번째는 더 따뜻하게.
- 게임 장면은 가볍게 시작해 *Have you ever... been scared of losing your home?*에서 분위기가 가라앉는다. 그 앞에서 한 박자 쉰다.
- 디에고의 소원은 작고 또렷하게. 마지막 대사는 조용히.

에스포지토 부인: 그게 핵심이에요. 좋아요. 촛불을 식탁에. 디에고, 성냥은 내 가방에 있어.

에스포지토 부인: 같은 노래. 매번. "대니 보이." 아주 천천히. 한 계단에 한 단어.

마리솔: 못 숨어, 디에고. 여기 옥상이야.
토미: 돌로레스? 에어컨은 어디 있어요? 고장 났다면서요… 우와.
