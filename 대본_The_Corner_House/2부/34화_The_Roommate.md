# THE CORNER HOUSE — 2부 34화

## The Roommate (룸메이트)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 2부 16화(통산 34화) · 시기: 2026년 8월 중순 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 고와너스, 제시의 새집 부엌
> - 등장인물: LEO, JESSE, MEGAN, TESS
> - 사건: 이사 2주 만의 첫 충돌. 설거지통의 머그컵, 재택근무 시간의 부엌 식탁, 테스의 외박 횟수, 폭염 때 나온 320달러 전기요금을 두고 제시와 레오가 부딪친다. 메건이 '하우스 회의'를 열어 규칙을 다시 정한다.
> - 학습 포인트: 생활 규칙 말하기, 비난과 변명(It's not my fault that… / That's partly my fault), 강조의 do(I did wash my mugs), 감정을 차분하게 말하기(It bothers me when…)
> - 미국 현안: 폭염 이후 늘어난 공과금과 분담 방식(균등 분담이 늘 공평하지는 않다)
> - 연결: 테스는 빵집 위층 원룸에 혼자 산다. 집안일 당번표, 냉장고 앞의 일정표. 제럴드의 세 번째 탈출(제시의 신발 속).

[Scene: Tuesday, 9:30 a.m. The kitchen. Leo stands by the sink with his green mug.]

LEO: Jesse. Hey. Do you have a minute before you start your day? I need to talk to you about something.
JESSE: Sure, Leo. What's up? You look really serious for a Tuesday morning.
LEO: It's the sink. It's full again, and I can't even fill the kettle for my tea.
JESSE: Okay... but those aren't my dishes, so I'm not sure why you're telling me.
LEO: They're not mine either, and I'm getting tired of washing everybody's stuff.
JESSE: Leo, that's your pan. It's the one with the eggs from yesterday, which you left there after breakfast.
LEO: The pan, okay, I'll give you the pan. But the mugs? There are four mugs in there, and those are yours.
JESSE: I did wash my mugs. I washed them last night, right before bed, because I always wash my mugs.
LEO: Then whose are they? They didn't walk into the sink by themselves.
JESSE: I don't know. Maybe they're Megan's, or maybe they're yours, because you drink tea all day long.
LEO: I drink tea from one mug, the green one. I wash it after every cup and use it again, so it's never in the sink.
JESSE: Then it's not my fault that there are four mugs in the sink.
LEO: Okay, fine, forget the mugs for now. There's another thing that's been bothering me, though.
JESSE: What else? Just say it, so we can get it all out.
LEO: It's the kitchen table. You were there all day yesterday with your laptop, and I had three calls.
JESSE: You told me I could use it when I moved in. Megan said we all share it, too.
LEO: We share it, yes. But you had papers everywhere, and you were typing so loud that I could hear it through my door.
JESSE: Typing loud? How do you type loud? It's a keyboard, not a drum.
LEO: You hit the keys like they owe you money.
JESSE: I was writing a complaint for my friend about his rent, and I had to get every number exactly right. It was important.
LEO: My calls were important too. My boss heard your typing, and she asked me if I was working in a factory.
JESSE: Okay, I'm sorry about that, I really am. I didn't know you had calls yesterday.
LEO: It's on the calendar on the fridge. I write all my calls there so that everybody knows when I need quiet.
JESSE: There's a calendar on the fridge? I've been opening that fridge every day for two weeks.
LEO: It's behind the takeout menus. It's been there since March.
JESSE: Nobody told me about the calendar, and I can't read through a pizza menu.
LEO: It's... I guess it's hidden. Okay. That's partly my fault.
JESSE: Partly.
LEO: Partly. Look, I'm not trying to fight with you. I just need quiet from ten to two on weekdays, because that's when most of my meetings are.
JESSE: Ten to two. I can go to the library then, or I can sit at the bar, since Tommy never minds.
LEO: Thanks. That would help a lot, and my boss will be very happy too.
JESSE: And can you do something for me in return?
LEO: What is it? Just tell me.
JESSE: Wash your egg pan before it becomes a science project, because I think something is growing in there.
LEO: It's soaking. Soaking is part of washing.
JESSE: It's been soaking for two days, Leo. The water is starting to change color.
LEO: It's a very dirty pan, so it needs a very long soak.
JESSE: Leo.
LEO: Okay, okay. I'll wash it now, before my first call starts.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **강조의 do** — 상대가 의심할 때 사실임을 강조한다. *I did wash my mugs.*(진짜로 씻었다니까) / *I do care.*
- **비난과 변명** — *It's not my fault that… / That's partly my fault. / My mistake. / I'm not trying to blame you.*
- **감정을 차분하게 말하기** — *It bothers me when…*(~할 때 신경 쓰여) 상대를 직접 공격하지 않고 상황을 주어로 말하는 방법.
- **생활 규칙** — *split three ways*(셋이 나누다), *two nights a week, max*, *a chart*(당번표), *dishes and trash*.
- **What's up?** — "무슨 일이야?"
- **like they owe you money** — "자판이 너한테 돈이라도 빌린 것처럼(세게)."
- **Soaking is part of washing.** — "불리는 것도 설거지의 일부야." 핑계의 전형.
- **a science project** — (곰팡이가 피어) 과학 실험이 되다. 농담.
- **Equal isn't always fair.** — "똑같은 게 늘 공평한 건 아니야."
- **Speak of the devil.** — "호랑이도 제 말 하면 온다더니."
- **I'm not a monster.** — "나 그렇게 나쁜 사람 아니야."
- **stay over** — 자고 가다.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **강조의 do는 크게**: *I **DID** wash my mugs.* — did에 강세, wash는 상대적으로 약하게.
- **partly** [파r틀리] — 레오가 인정할 때 작게, 제시가 받아칠 때 *Partly.*를 또렷하게.
- 언쟁 장면은 짧은 대사를 빠르게 주고받다가, 메건의 *Okay. Stop. Both of you.*에서 속도를 확 늦춘다.
- **It bothers me when…** — 회의 규칙대로 차분하게, 감정을 눌러서.
- **reptile** [렙타일], **developer** [디**벨**러퍼r], **kindergarten** [**킨**더r가r튼].
- *Speak of the devil.*은 장난스럽게 작게. 메건의 *Leo!*는 바로 끊는다.
