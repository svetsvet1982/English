## Vacation Plans (휴가 계획)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 2부 10화(통산 28화) · 시기: 2026년 7월 · 난이도: B1~B2 · 턴 수: 120
> - 장소: 바 The Hook (영업 전 테이블)
> - 등장인물: TOMMY, TASHA, MARCUS, JESSE, GINA, EDDIE
> - 사건: 썩은 바닥 공사로 8월 13~16일 바가 문을 닫는다. 유급 휴가가 생긴 직원과 친구들이 푸에르토리코 해변이냐 캐츠킬 캠핑이냐로 논쟁한다. 제시의 면허증이 REAL ID가 아니라는 사실이 결정타가 된다.
> - 학습 포인트: 2형 가정법(If we were…, we would… / If the flight were canceled…), 선택·타협(Why don't we…? / What if we…?), 비용 비교
> - 미국 현안: 항공 지연(관제사 부족), REAL ID 시행(2025년 5월부터 국내선 탑승에 필요), 여름 성수기 항공권 가격
> - 연결: 바닥 수리비는 리치가 "지금은 안 된다"고 해서 토미가 부담. 제시는 8월 1일 고와너스 입주 예정. 토미는 주말 이틀만 버스로 합류하기로 → 에디가 바닥 공사 감독. 내년 푸에르토리코 약속.

[Scene: Tuesday, 10:30 a.m. The Hook, before opening. Tommy stands behind the bar. The floor creaks.]

TOMMY: Everybody, listen up for a second, because I have news. There's good news, and there's bad news.
TASHA: Bad news first. Always bad news first, so the good news can fix it.
TOMMY: The floor behind the bar is falling apart. The wood underneath is rotten, because water has been getting in for years.
MARCUS: I noticed that last week. It bounces when you walk on it, like a trampoline.
TOMMY: So I have to close for four days in August, from Thursday the thirteenth to Sunday the sixteenth.
JESSE: Four whole days? And who pays for the new floor? Isn't that the landlord's job?
TOMMY: I asked Richie, and he said, "Not now." So I'm paying for it myself, like always.
GINA: Dad, how much is it going to cost?
TOMMY: Don't ask. But here's the good news. You all get four days off, and for Tasha and Jesse, they're paid.
TASHA: Paid days off? Tommy, are you feeling okay? Do you have a fever or something?
TOMMY: I'm not sick, I'm tired. I've gone twenty years without a real vacation, so at least you should have one.
JESSE: And what about you? Where are you going to go?
TOMMY: I'll be right here, watching the floor guys. Somebody has to make sure they do it right.
GINA: Dad, you should come with us this time, wherever we end up going.
TOMMY: So where are you going?
GINA: I don't know yet, and that's the problem. Everyone wants something completely different.
MARCUS: I know exactly where we should go. Camping, up in the Catskills.
TASHA: Camping? With bugs? No, thank you. I want a beach, a real beach, and that means Puerto Rico.
TOMMY: And there it is. The fight begins.
GINA: Okay, let's do this like a meeting at work. Everyone makes their case, and nobody interrupts.

[Scene: The same table. Coffee cups. Gina takes notes on a napkin.]

TASHA: Me first. My Tía Lourdes has a house in Rincón, on the west coast, and we can stay there for free. We just need flights.
MARCUS: Flights in August, though? Do you know what the prices are like right now?
TASHA: I checked this morning. It's about four fifty round trip from JFK.
JESSE: Four fifty! Is that for each person?
TASHA: Each. But the house is free, remember? Free! The food is cheap, and the water is warm, not like Rockaway.
GINA: Honestly, that sounds amazing. I can already feel the sun on my face.

JESSE: And what about the campsite? I'm guessing those aren't free either.

GINA: So camping would be... maybe a hundred and fifty each? For all four days?
MARCUS: About that, and that includes food, because we cook everything on a fire.
TASHA: But that means I would be sleeping on the ground. With spiders.
MARCUS: You'd be sleeping on a mat, a very nice mat. And the spiders up there are small.

## 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **2형 가정법 (If + 과거형, would + 동사원형)** — 지금 사실이 아니거나 일어날 가능성이 낮은 상상.
  - *If we were in Rincón right now, we would be watching the sunset.*
  - *If the flight were canceled, we would lose a whole day of vacation.* (격식체·교과서에서는 주어와 상관없이 were)
  - *If I were young, I would carry all the bags.*
  - *If you came for just two days, would the world end?*
- **1형과의 차이** — *If it rains, we go to the diner in town.*(실제로 그럴 수 있는 일, 현재형) vs *If it were canceled…*(가정).
- **타협 표현** — *Let's look for a compromise. / Why don't we do both? / What if we go camping this time? / We're even.*(피장파장이네)
- **make (one's) case** — 자기 주장을 펴다.
- **Keep talking.** — "계속해 봐(마음에 드는데)."
- **That settles it.** — "그걸로 결정 났네."
- **split four ways** — 넷이서 나누다.
- **round trip / domestic flight / REAL ID / DMV** — 왕복 / 국내선 / 연방 기준 신분증(별 표시) / 차량국.
- **I'll think about it. / You always say that.** — 미루는 대답과 그걸 꿰뚫는 반응.
- **supervise the supervisor** — 감독을 감독하다. 에디의 농담.

## 낭독 포인트 (낭독하지 않음)

- **가정법의 would는 'd로 줄어든다**: *we'd fly* [위드 플라이], *I'd have to* [아이드 해프 투]. *we would be watching*은 상상이므로 꿈꾸듯 천천히.
- **If I were young, | I would carry all the bags.** — 조건절 끝을 살짝 올리고 쉰다.
- **Rincón** [린콘], **Catskills** [캣스킬즈], **s'mores** [스모어r즈](모닥불에 구운 마시멜로 과자).
- **REAL ID** [리얼 아이디], **DMV** [디엠비] — 약어는 글자마다.
- 타샤와 마커스의 홍보 대결은 같은 문형(*If we were in…, we would be watching…*)으로 맞받아친다. 리듬을 맞춰 읽는다.
- 토미의 *...Probably not.*은 마지못해 작게. 이 화의 반전.
