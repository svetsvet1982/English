- **Do the math.** — 계산해 봐(뻔하잖아) (034)

- **He said that ~** — (간접화법) 그가 ~라고 했다 / "He said that if I talk to Hale, I'll never work in this city again" (056)
- **if you'd understood ~, there wouldn't be ~** — (혼합 가정법) 과거에 ~했다면 지금 ~는 없을 텐데 / "if you'd understood that, there wouldn't be a story" (084)

- **speak through counsel** — 변호인을 통해 말하다 (086)
- **accept the terms** — 조건을 수용하다 / "Vanta accepts our terms." (097)
