## 핵심 표현 정리 (낭독하지 않음)

- **pull something off** — (어려운 일을) 해내다 / "Can we really pull this off?" (004)
- **on a shoestring** — 아주 적은 예산으로 / "I threw Walter's fiftieth on a shoestring." (005)
- **RSVP** — 참석 회신 / "Two hundred and twelve RSVPs." (008)
- **Wouldn't miss it** — 꼭 갈게, 절대 안 빠져 / "Wouldn't miss it. Let's talk scale." (013)
- **at cost** — 원가로 / "Three hundred sliders. SPK. At cost." (017)
- **stay out of someone's way** — ~을 방해하지 않다 / "I stay out of Rita's way." (033)
- **final final** — 진짜 최종본 (구어) / "Final? Like, final final?" (040, 041)
- **for old times' sake** — 옛정을 봐서 / "Leave it. For old times' sake." (048)
- **filler** — (빈자리를 채우려고 넣는) 땜빵 / "No filler." (060, 118)
- **make it a thing** — 유난 떨다, 일을 크게 만들다 / "Don't make it a thing." (074, 075)
- **close the deal** — 거래를 마무리하다 / "Dallas closed the deal on Vanta New York today." (111)
- **Your call** — 네가 결정할 일이야 / "Your call, partner." (118)
